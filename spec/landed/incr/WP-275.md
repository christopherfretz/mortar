# WP-275: the state-format change for phase B, in ONE VERSION bump (DESIGN)

A design record of stored state. It changes no code. Base 1bfce906
(main 2064dd12 plus WP-267 P-1c), branch `wp/275-state-format`. The
brief's five data, by the brief's numbers:
1. the update's `.symtab` plan from rows (255e5);
2. the chunk -> (file, region) index (255e4 rulings items 4/5);
3. WP-270: packed chunks' members (`Why::Packed`);
4. 255h: a `RESOLVED` row in a merge section -> its `FRAGS` fragment
   and offset;
5. R-8: `RESOLVED.facts` written by the update itself.

Status of each number: **measured** (with its source) or **estimate**
(with its model). The verifier is named through `spec/plumb.md` only.
The evidence is in `.build/target-wp-143/w275/` (scripts `st5.py`, a
reader of a VERSION 12 state by its directory, and `mapcount.py`, counts
from an output and its map).

`seq` is a position key with gaps (the capture writes `k << 32` for the
k-th unit of a chunk in offset order; an update inserting between two
rows takes their midpoint, IN-18e's rule for `FILES.rank`; no midpoint
left: compaction). No row holds an offset: a unit's offset is read from
its `REGIONS` or `SECTIONS` row, so a growth step (which shifts offsets
and keeps the order) rewrites no `CHUNK_UNITS` row.

Not new rows, answered in this record: WP-274's D-14 (a merged chunk's
fragments) is a carry (`LayoutDelta::frags` plus the changed files'
pieces), because no fragment of an unchanged file ever moves (IN-72
item 6); see §2.6.

## 2. The rows

### 2.1 Item 1: no row; the remap rule (proposed IN-49a text)

> An unchanged file's block is remapped from the previous output's
> entries. An entry whose section index names an allocated section in
> which this update moved units by different amounts (a packed chunk it
> re-packed, IN-72 item 4; `.eh_frame` ranges it moved, IN-34a) moves by
> the delta of the unit of THAT FILE that holds its old value: its member
> of the packed chunk (`CHUNK_UNITS`), or its `.eh_frame` range (`FILES`
> eh columns before and after). Every other entry moves by its output
> section's delta. An entry that two units of its file hold (a value at
> the end of one and the start of the next) with different deltas is
> refused `update-not-implemented (symtab)`.

Readers: U7/U9's `.symtab` writer (`write_symtab`'s remap, after the
moves): for each moved non-uniform unit (file f, old range, delta), f's
`SYMTAB` block, its entries with that section index in the old range.
O(moved units' blocks). Writer: none. Stale: §6.

### 2.2 Items 2 and 3: `CHUNK_UNITS` (kind 58)

- **Content (a pure function of the state's own rows):** for every
  `Body` chunk, its units in offset order. A hole-tolerant allocated chunk
  (the chunks `REGIONS` covers) has one unit per `REGIONS` row placed in
  it (`unit` 0). Every other `Body` chunk (packed, `None`-class, non-alloc
  non-debug, debug) has one unit per section whose `SECTIONS` place is in
  it, any size (`unit` 1; NOBITS and zero-size included, the re-pack
  needs them). Ties at one offset (zero-size): by (`FILES.rank`, `local`).
  No other chunk kind has rows (merged: §2.6; synthetic, patches,
  structure: no input units).
- **Layout:** `chunk u32` (`CHUNKS` row), `seq u64`, `file u32`
  (`FILES` row), `local u32`, `unit u8`, `tomb u8` = 22 bytes; sorted by
  `(chunk, seq)`.
- **Writer (capture):** `capture.rs`, a new `chunk_unit_rows(m:
  &MemoryState)` after `region_rows`, over the same rows; O(placed
  sections) plus a sort per chunk. `compact` renumbers `seq` to `k << 32`.
- **Readers:** `UpdateRows::members` (U8's dirty-chunk files, U9's
  `Members` for `clip_plan::for_clips`; `check_member_counts` unchanged):
  per clip chunk, binary search by offset over its rows, each probe's
  offset read from the unit's `REGIONS` or `SECTIONS` row, then the met
  units (plus the gap rule's predecessor and next with bytes), then the
  met files' `SECTIONS` ranges for unit-0 rows. WP-270's re-pack in
  `delta::layout` (`Why::Packed`): every row of the packed chunk, their
  `SECTIONS` rows, their files' `FILES.rank`. §2.1's remap. WP-273's
  window-neighbour row once front 2 is gone (§8).
- **Patched or written whole:** patched (key-keyed). A unit appears
  (a revived or new section of a non-region chunk, a file's new region,
  an appeared or extracted file): inserted with the midpoint `seq` of its
  offset-order neighbours. A unit goes (killed, a gone or unextracted
  file, a region closed): tombstone. A unit's order changes (a region or
  member moved past a neighbour, a re-pack that reorders): tombstone and
  insert. A unit's `local` changes (its file's `SECTIONS` or `REGIONS`
  range re-indexed by a new version): the row rewritten in place. A
  growth step, or a re-pack that keeps the order: no row.
- **Capacity:** the key-keyed class (append area max(4,096 rows,
  rows/64)); no midpoint left: compaction (IN-18f).
- **`canon`:** per chunk the ordered list of (unit, file by load-order
  position, the local by its canonical name: a region by (output section,
  category, ordinal), a section by (file, section index)); `seq` is
  forgotten.

### 2.3 Why `seq` and no offset

A growth step shifts offsets inside a chunk's followers, and the delta's
re-pack shifts members, but neither changes the ORDER. An offset column
would be a second copy of `REGIONS.offset`/`SECTIONS.place_offset`
rewritten on every such step (the stale-datum class); the order alone is
rewritten only when it changes. The cost is a binary search that reads
one `REGIONS` or `SECTIONS` row per probe (about 15 probes for 24,000
rows).

### 2.4 Item 4: `MERGE_DEFS` (kind 59)

- **Content:** one row per NAMED definition of a global key in a
  `Merge` section by a live (extracted) file: every such `MENTIONS` row
  (def kind section), not only the winner's, so a winner that moves to an
  unchanged file (a strong definition removed) finds its piece without
  opening the file. Locals have no row (an unchanged file's locals move
  with their fragment's chunk, which moves whole).
- **Layout:** `file u32`, `local u32` (the section's position in the
  file's `SECTIONS` range), `value u64` (`st_value`), `out u32`, `class
  u32`, `hash [u8;16]` (the `FRAGS` key of the piece's fragment),
  `within u32`, `ordinal u32` (`PieceRef`), `tomb u8` = 49 bytes; sorted
  by `(file, local, value)`.
- **Writer (capture):** `capture.rs`/`tables.rs`, from the link: for each
  such mention, `Rows::named_piece(s, value)` of the full link (the
  image's own function) and the fragment's identity. A piece whose
  fragment has no `FRAGS` row (never placed: collected) keeps its
  identity; the reader then sees it not live (`named_piece_dead`).
- **Readers:** `StoredRows::named_piece(s, v)`
  (`crates/mortar/src/update/stored_rows.rs`:419): `s` -> its file and
  local (`SECTIONS` row, `FILE_INDEX`), one binary search, then `FRAGS` by
  the key (two binary searches, IN-72 item 6's lookup). Used by U7's
  values (`value_in`: `DEFINED`/`SYM_PLACE`), U9's relocations through the
  key, `symtab_incr::plan`'s `GlobalChange` for a global in a split
  section.
- **Patched:** a changed, appeared or extracted file: its rows deleted
  (the `(file)` prefix) and its new ones inserted from its parse; a gone
  or unextracted file: deleted. Nothing else changes a row (a function of
  the file's bytes and its output section; an output-section change is a
  full link).
- **Capacity:** the key-keyed class. **`canon`:** a section by (file,
  section index), a file by load-order position.

### 2.5 Item 5: no row; the update's writer of `RESOLVED.facts`

The update writes `facts` for: every key a changed file mentions (its new
and its stored `MENTIONS`), every key whose definer changed, and every
key defined in a section whose liveness U6 flipped (`now_live`,
`now_dead`: the definer files' `MENTIONS` ranges, restricted to those
sections). The last set is not inside the first: a death that cascades
(c.o stops calling `u_helper`, whose section held the only call to
`v_helper`) flips `dead` on a key no changed file mentions, which P-3b's
binding check (`facts_within_changed`) would refuse. Bits: `dead` = the
definer's section has no place (the scan's rule, `view.rs`:174);
`comdat_only`, `gnu_warning` as the scan computes them. A carry of U5/U6,
written by U10's patch (`RESOLVED` key-parallel edits).

### 2.6 WP-274's D-14 (a merged chunk's fragments): a carry

IN-72 item 6: "No fragment of an unchanged file ever moves". The bytes
an update writes in a merged chunk are new identities (the tail), revived
fragments ("rewritten from the piece") and dead constants (filled). Each
comes from a changed, appeared or drawn-in file's pieces (`FilePieces`,
in memory) or is fill. Proposed sentence for IN-72 item 6: "C''s index of
a merged chunk is the fragments of `LayoutDelta::frags` that this update
writes, each with the changed file's piece it is copied from; every other
byte of the chunk is the previous output's." `FragMember`'s size and
alignment are in `FRAGS` (`size`, the class word's `align`). Nothing
stored.

## 3. The VERSION bump (12 -> 13)

- `state5/mod.rs`: `VERSION = 13`, the doc line "13 since WP-275
  (`CHUNK_UNITS` and `MERGE_DEFS` written by the full link's capture: a
  version-12 state holds neither, which the member index would read as a
  chunk without units and `named_piece` as a definition without a piece;
  it is refused, `state-version`)"; `kind::CHUNK_UNITS = 58`,
  `kind::MERGE_DEFS = 59`; `TABLES` 38 -> 40 (key-keyed both); `name()`.
- **An old state is refused, not converted.** `MERGE_DEFS` needs the
  inputs' bytes (the split), which `incr_state convert` does not read;
  `CHUNK_UNITS` could be derived, but one table converted and one not is
  two rules for one bump. The next link after the upgrade is a full link
  (as for 6..12).
- **Tests (255g2's pattern):**
  - `state5_columns::chunk_units_and_merge_defs_survive_the_patch_and_the_compaction`:
    encode/decode both tables; a PATCH that inserts a unit by midpoint
    between two rows, tombstones one, rewrites a `local`; the compaction
    (seq renumbered `k << 32`, order kept); a VERSION 12 state refused
    `state-version`.
  - `state_producers::the_chunk_units_are_the_derived_index` (a64, x64):
    for a full link, `CHUNK_UNITS` equals the index derived from every
    file's `SECTIONS`/`REGIONS` (today's O(sections) rule, as the
    reference), and per `Body` chunk the unit-1 rows plus the sections of
    its unit-0 regions match `CHUNKS.members` under `is_clip_member`.
  - `state_producers::merge_defs_are_the_full_links_pieces` (a64, x64): a
    global in `.rodata.str1.1` and one in `.rodata.cst4` of a file, plus a
    weak copy in a second file: one row each, `(frag key, within,
    ordinal)` equal to the link's `named_piece`.
  - `incr_state` canon: two states that differ only in `seq` compare
    equal; a reorder compares different.

## 6. Adversarial points: how each datum goes stale, and its judge

The lane's (d0) (`tests/l10/judge.py`, `incr_state compare` of S' with
G, IN-78) compares against G, a FRESH full link of the hop's inputs. It
is exact only on an identity hop whose base was a full link
(`d0_exact`, judge.py:765). On every other hop it excludes the
placement tables (`D0_PLACEMENT_TABLES`, judge.py:697: `CHUNKS`,
`ORDER`, `HOLES`, `REGIONS`, `SYMTAB`, ...) and the place columns of
`SECTIONS`, because the update's placement may lawfully differ from
G's fresh one (WP-267 review §6). So:
- `CHUNK_UNITS` is placement (its order and presence follow the
  update's placement, not G's). On a non-identity hop (d0) CANNOT judge
  it. Proposed: add `CHUNK_UNITS` to `D0_PLACEMENT_TABLES` with P-275a;
  otherwise every hop that places differently from G fails (d0) by
  construction. Its every-hop judge is the derived-index check: the
  table is DERIVED from `SECTIONS` and `REGIONS` of the same state, so
  `incr_state check` (proposed; `incr_state` has no `check` subcommand
  today) re-derives it from S' and compares, with
  no full link. The lane runs it on every S'. The judge against a full
  link is (d) LIVE (WP-274 B-10: a replay of the map's P_U).
  The derived-index check catches a `CHUNK_UNITS` row that disagrees
  with S''s own `SECTIONS`/`REGIONS`; it does NOT catch those place rows
  being stale themselves (WP-274 R-3): that is (d) LIVE's, and the next
  hops' (b).
- `MERGE_DEFS` and `RESOLVED.facts` are not placement: their fields
  come from the inputs and the resolution (`FRAGS` keys are content
  hashes; `within`/`ordinal` are the piece's in its file). (d0)
  compares them on EVERY hop.

| datum | goes stale when | judge |
|---|---|---|
| `CHUNK_UNITS` order | a region or member moves past a neighbour (hole reuse, the tail, a re-pack that reorders) and the row is not moved | the derived-index check on every S'; the reader's probe check (a probed unit's own row places it in a different chunk or out of order: refused by name); (d0) only on an identity hop; (d) LIVE (B-10) |
| `CHUNK_UNITS.local` | a changed or drawn-in file's `SECTIONS`/`REGIONS` range re-indexed (a section added before others) and its rows kept | the derived-index check (the re-derived `local` differs); (d) LIVE |
| `CHUNK_UNITS` presence | a unit killed (gc), revived, a file gone or appeared, and no tombstone or insert | the derived-index check; `check_member_counts` (count vs list) on a whole chunk; (d) LIVE |
| `MERGE_DEFS` | its file changes or goes, its rows kept | (d0) (rows recomputed from the inputs); the reader's check (the `FRAGS` lookup must find a row of the same out and class, else refused by name) |
| `MERGE_DEFS` (winner moved) | a strong definition removed, the weak one in an unchanged file wins | not stale by construction: every live definition has a row; (d0) |
| `RESOLVED.facts` (R-8) | a key's facts flip with no changed file mentioning it (a gc cascade, a COMDAT keep moving) and the update does not recompute it | (d0) (`RESOLVED` recomputed); P-3b's binding check refuses in the update itself today |
| item 1 (no row) | an unchanged block entry left at its old value when its unit moved non-uniformly | IN-79 (a) equivalence and (b) the verifier on the output's `.symtab`; NOT (d0), which compares `SYMTAB` rows, not entry values (named, not checked: §9) |
| D-14 (no row) | a fragment of an unchanged file moved by the update | cannot happen by IN-72 item 6; (b) and (a) on the bytes |
