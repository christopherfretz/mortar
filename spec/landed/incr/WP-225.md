### 0.1 The decision that needs a ruling first: what `Link` the refill gets

`mortar_image::update::refill` (`crates/mortar_image/src/update.rs:135`)
and `mortar_image::map::update_map` (`map.rs:922`) take the IMAGE's
`Link<'_, '_>` (`crates/mortar_image/src/link.rs:257`): `inputs` (every
file's parsed model), `resolution`, `assignment`, `liveness`, `split`,
`order`, `records`, `demands`, `layout`, `eh: (&EhPlan, &dyn EhSource)`,
`synth`, `facts`, `proof`, `recheck`, `fixups`, `args`. Every field is a
WHOLE-PROGRAM structure of a front; no function builds one from a
format-5 state plus the changed files. U-6's own tests
(`crates/mortar/tests/update_image.rs`) build it from "a second front
over the edited inputs" (its module doc), and WP-218 §C lists what the
image's table builders read of every unchanged file (every placed
section, symbol, fragment, fixup, the eh plan). So the callee FORCES a
whole front: IN-18c's defect class, and the largest one.

Two ways, both named so the ruling can pick:

- **(A) v1: the whole front over the new inputs, its layout replaced by
  the materialized `P_U`** (the front's replay hook, IN-61, `front::Env::
  replay`, `crates/mortar/src/front.rs:296/1189`; the floor's own
  architecture, `crates/mortar/src/floor.rs`). The U-steps run on the
  `StateView` as the cut says (they decide the placement, the refill
  set, the symtab plan, the patch); the front supplies the image's
  `Link`, and the per-file producers of U4 (0.3, G-2) read the changed
  files' rows from that front with the capture's own functions (one
  producer for a full link and an update: the stale-datum class is
  narrowed to the delta's own decisions). Correct soonest; every
  milestone M2-M5 is judged on it. **It cannot meet the go/no-go**: the
  front is most of a full link (spec/incr.md §1.5, protoc without `-g`,
  120.7 ms: input 57.7, resolve 8.4, assignment 4.1, split 3.5, scan
  4.4, synthetic 3.3, layout 21.8, image 13.5 ms; WP-244: plain protoc
  0.23-0.24 s), so an (A) update is a full front + the delta's steps +
  the materialization + the refill: above a plain full link. M6's table
  would record the miss as a FINDING with its phases, as the brief
  allows, but it is decided now by construction, not by measurement.
- **(B) the lazy `Link`**: a `Link` whose unchanged files are backed by
  the state's rows and parsed on demand (only the units the dirty
  windows reach: a window is filled whole, image.md I-95, so unchanged
  units overlapping a dirty window are parsed and relocated too), plus
  a per-file front (parse, split, scan of the files of the change
  alone, the scan's `KeyFacts` from `RESOLVED.facts` and the delta).
  That is a change of `mortar_image`'s `Link`/`LinkTables`/`layout_plan`
  and of the facade's front: not mine, and larger than the join (my
  estimate: L, design first).

**Proposal**: (A) for this package, with every U4 adapter and the
`Link` behind ONE function of `mortar::update` (`fn image_link(..)`),
so that (B) replaces that function and the U4 producers without
touching the orchestration's order; (B) cut now as its own package
(design step, then code) so the go/no-go is taken on (B). (A) also
gives an internal cross-check for free in tests: the whole front's
resolution against `ResolveDelta`, its capture against the update's
patch (the in-crate (d), 0.4 G-11).
