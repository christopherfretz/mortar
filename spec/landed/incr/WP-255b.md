# WP-255b: the image's kernels on `Rows` (a refactor; no byte changes)

Base c71e12e5 (batch 16). Design: `spec/landed/incr/WP-255.md` §3 (C'), §9.1, §9.5, §9.8.

## What moved where
- `crates/mortar_image/src/rows.rs` (new):
  - `trait Rows`: the leaf reads.
  - `FrontRows<'a, 's>`: the trait over the `Link`. It holds the fields `LinkTables` had: the symbol tables, the slot indexes, the GOT addresses, the output sections, the piece index and the per-global value cache.
  - The formulas, generic over `R`, moved out of `link.rs` (the old bodies are deleted): `value` (was `sym_value_of`), `sym`, `def_value`, `entry_value`, `value_in`, `addr_in_placed`, `named_piece_dead`, `kept_copy_value` (its conditions are `Rows::kept_copy`), `address`, `alloc_addr`, `chunk_at`, `frag_value`, `def_entry`, `plt` and `slot` (the slot address).
  - `Valued`, `OutSection` and `out_sections` moved here from `link.rs`.
  - `RowTables<R>`: `SymNames`, `FillTables` and `fill::Tables` are implemented once, generically. That covers `record_sym`, `record_sym_at`, `section_writable`, `target_alloc`, `split_target`, `split_place` and `split_at`.
  - `row_for::<R>` and `SectionFront`: the `Input` row maker, moved out of `from_layout`.
- `link.rs`:
  - `pub type LinkTables<'a, 's> = RowTables<FrontRows<'a, 's>>`. The impls that stay specialized to the full link are `RecordKeys`, `SlotValues`, `SymAddrs`, the map, the text map and `DynView`. They reach `FrontRows` through `Deref`.
  - `structure(link)` builds `StructureData` and `ShdrData` and calls `write_structure`. `section_headers_of(&ShdrData)` replaces `section_headers(link)`. The refusals stay in the same order: no file header, a section header, `e_phnum`, then `e_flags`.
- `layout_plan.rs`:
  - `from_layout<R: Rows>(m, rows, …)` now takes the rows. Its one caller is `with_image`, which passes `tables.rows()`.
  - `pads(Vec<ChunkView>, &[SegView], target, separate_code, b)` is now public. `pad_views(m)` builds the two lists.
- `.eh_frame_hdr`: its writer is `mortar_synth`'s `HdrChunk { plan, eh, src }`, which already reads plans and not the `Link`. It is unchanged.

No call site outside `mortar_image` changed. `map.rs` and `update.rs` are untouched; `LinkTables::new` keeps its signature.

## `Rows` against §9.5
The trait is the leaf reads that the moved formulas really make. Every method is answered by what the full link already had.
- **Added:**
  - `out_section`: `chunk_at` for a non-alloc chunk.
  - `section_kind`: Merge and `.eh_frame` dispatch.
  - `section_place`: P-1.
  - `out_flags`: D-46.
  - `kept_copy`: I-47a.
  - `named_piece`, `split_row`, `section_piece`, `frag_live`, `frag_place`, `frag_offset`, `merged_chunk`, `outside_merge`: §9.3's merge globals and S-17/S-18. They replace §9.5's single `merge_place`.
  - `eh_value` and `eh_frame`: S-50.
  - `entry`: an `EntryDef`, the resolved definition. It replaces `def(k)`'s symbol half.
  - `def(SymbolId) -> Option<Def>`.
  - `global`: the per-global cache. `FrontRows` fills it in parallel with `def_value::<FrontRows>`; a state source can compute it on demand.
  - `alloc_place` and `copy_of`: they replace `sym_place`.
  - `facts`, `facts_at` and `name`.
  - `got` and `got_addrs`: the slot offsets.
  - `slot_index`: §9.5's `slot`, returning an index.
  - `redirect`: returns `(chunk, offset)`.
  - `tls`.
- **Not added, because no kernel of 255b reads them through `Rows`:** `segments`, `outsec_name`, `synth_chunk`, `sections_of`, `out_of`, `holes` and `patches`. `pads` and the structural writers take data lists instead.
- **Signatures that differ from §9.5:**
  - `value::<R>(r, key, split, facts) -> (SymValue, bool)`. The fill needs the split flag and the facts at a mention.
  - `row_for::<R>(r, s, (chunk, offset), &SectionFront, proof, b)`. `FileFront` is 255d's, and `SectionFront` is what the maker reads of it.
  - `RowTables<R>` owns its `R`.

## Plain-link cost (AArch64 host; release builds of base c71e12e5 and head fc9fd3bd)
11 interleaved pairs per workload, alternating which binary runs first. Wall and maximum RSS come from `/usr/bin/time`, whose resolution is 10 ms. The 1-minute load was 7.0 to 11.5.

| workload | base median (IQR) | head median (IQR) | RSS base / head |
|---|---|---|---|
| protoc, no `-g` (cxx-medium-static) | 0.130 s (0.12-0.14) | 0.130 s (0.13-0.14) | 146 / 146 MB |
| protoc, `-g` (cxx-medium-g-static) | 0.230 s (0.23-0.25) | 0.230 s (0.23-0.24) | 249 / 249 MB |
| clang `-g` (cxx-large-g) | 2.84 s (2.36-4.04) | 2.69 s (2.46-4.36) | 1969 / 1970 MB |

No difference shows above the noise: 10 ms on protoc, and an IQR of about 1.7 s on clang `-g`, which is load-bound.

`cargo xtask bench --check --linker mortar` with head's binary, on the three workloads: `unsteady: the run is noisy` (calibration 27.5 ms before, 44.7 ms after). protoc, both variants: `improved` against the 2026-11-15 baseline. clang `-g`: `new: the baseline does not have this link`. The verifier passed all 3 outputs clean, and all 3 programs behave the same as GNU ld's. The bench also printed `OUTPUT CHANGED` on both protoc variants against that baseline's hash. The baseline predates base c71e12e5, and base and head write byte-identical outputs on both workloads (`cmp`: 11,752,064 and 50,306,704 bytes), so the change is not this package's.
