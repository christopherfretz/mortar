## 0. The shape in one paragraph

Front 1 does four jobs: (i) it is the SOURCE of U4's rows (`changed_files`
is the capture's per-file makers run over front 1's link), (ii) it is the
JUDGE of U4-U7 (U5 vs its capture, `live_judged` vs its mark, `judge` vs
its layout, `facts_into` vs its `RESOLVED`, G-3/G-4 vs its bytes), (iii) in
the identity case it is the IMAGE and the STATE written (its refill and
its capture), and (iv) its `Front` is what `materialize_on` translates
into (front ids). Front 2 already does (iii) for a non-identity hop, with
the same inputs, plan and store. So the removal is two phases:

- **Phase A (this package's cut; before and alongside C')**: U4-U7 from
  the state and the changed files alone (no `Front`); EVERY taken update
  runs ONE front, front 2, with the update's placement replayed (the
  identity case replays the stored placement); `materialize` and the
  judges move from front 1 into front 2 (same inputs, so the same
  answers), where they stay BINDING; front 1 survives only as a
  findings-only shadow under `MORTAR_UPDATE_FINDINGS` (the old
  `reference_of`, every judge as today, never on the real path).
- **Phase B (C' replaces front 2, 255e4 on, plus the state patch)**: the
  judges that front 2 carries become findings-only shadows too; the real
  path's guards are the update's own refusals by name, `Layout::check`
  on `P_U`, and in the lane IN-79 (a)-(d) with (d) LIVE (§2.5).

Removed by phase A (255e3's laps): front 84 + refill 22 + capture 114 +
identity 156 + front1-drop 15 = **391 ms**, less what moves (materialize,
~70 ms, moves into front 2; it is not new). §3 has the table.

### 2.2 Placed-as-computed (new, phase A, real path)

In front 2's replay hook, after materialize: every section is placed in
`lu` exactly when front 2's `computed` places it (O(sections), inside a
front that is O(link) already). It subsumes `live_judged`'s set equality
(a front places exactly its kept live sections) and `judge`'s first
property, for unchanged files too (today's `judge` checks only the
changed files' sections, `mod.rs:2106-2122`). A disagreement: FINDING,
`(layout)` by name.

### 2.3 identity' (M-2)

Today identity is "canon(capture) − canon(stored) ⊆ {STAT, LEAVES, the
changed files' FILES rows}" (`mod.rs:2224-2231`). identity' is the same
predicate over the rows an update can change, which IN-78's derived table
lists by cause: the changed files' ranges and key-keyed rows (P-1's
`ChangedFile` against the stored ranges, row by row, `facts` included),
the affected keys' `SYMBOLS`/`RESOLVED`/`KEY_REFS` (the resolve delta
against the stored rows), and the layout rows (the layout delta: no
`moved`, no `growth_steps`, no new `FRAGS`, every changed section at its
stored place and size, no liveness flip, the `.symtab` block's counts and
the `.eh_frame` range unchanged). O(change). Anything identity' cannot
judge is "not identity". Its shadow is the old check (§2.1).

### 2.4 The verifier's promise, and a finding

`spec/plumb.md` §1 (stage I row, line 40): "`--equiv` (incr.md IN-62) is
not started". The lane calls `xtask plumb --equiv` (`tests/l10/judge.py:511-515`),
but by the verifier's SPEC only stages 1/2/x/I are promised (placement,
relocations, symbol values, content against the inputs; holes and dead
ranges; `.symtab` blocks). The design relies on those, not on
`--equiv`. **Finding F-1**: plumb.md's stage I row should say what
`--equiv` checks today, or the lane's `equiv` check should be named "not
a spec check" (proposed sentence for plumb.md §1: "`--equiv` compares
…; until IN-62 is specified it is a lane check, not a stage").

### 2.5 IN-79 (d) is a stub: the guard phase B needs

`tests/l10/judge.py:589-600`: `CanonStub.compare` returns "(d) not run
(canon: U-2)", and `placement_of_map` returns "P_U: decision records not
read" (`:606-610`). Today every state an update writes is a front's
capture, so IN-78 holds by construction; phase A keeps that (front 2's
capture). Phase B does not: the state patch is the update's own rows,
and nothing in the lane would see a stale one. `mortar_incr::state5::canon`

state), exact on an identity hop, and on any hop equal outside IN-78's
decision rows and the derived layout rows. (d0) must be live before
phase B removes front 2; it is worth starting now (P-4).

## 5. The order against C'

- **Before C'** (recommended, P-1..P-5 alongside 255e4): saves ~391 ms
  now. Throw-away when C' later removes front 2: P-2's move of
  materialize into the hook (~the body of `materialize_on` and its call)
  and placed-as-computed (both front-id work). P-1, P-1b, P-3, P-4 are
  NOT thrown away: C''s `shadow_set` already reads `ChangedFiles` built
  by front 1 (`mod.rs:1228`, inside `placement`), so C' on the real path
  needs P-1/P-3 anyway.
- **After C'**: no P-2 throw-away, but C' would have to take the identity
  hop as well (today front 1 writes it), and C' cannot be on the real
  path before 255e4 (S3 bytes), 255h, the state patch and IN-75a: the
  391 ms stay for that whole time, and C''s shadow keeps judging against
  front 2 only.
- **Alongside** is what the cut above allows: the only shared function is
  `front_two`, split by ownership.
