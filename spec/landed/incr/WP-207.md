# WP-207: the incremental update, second design pass (after R-C and R-L)

The update designed by WP-202 (`spec/landed/incr/WP-202.md`, `spec/incr.md`
§5.10) was attacked before any code by two adversarial reviews:
`briefs/reviews/review-in77.md` (R-C: writes, crash protocol, patched
state; 5 blockers, 9 majors, 8 minors) and `briefs/reviews/review-in72.md`
(R-L: delta resolution and layout, referrers, symbol tables; 4 blockers,
8 majors, 7 minors). This record disposes of all 41 findings, states the
rules as amended in `spec/incr.md` (ids stable; new ids are letter
suffixes), the cut and the test plan that now bind, and the requests to
other specs. Every claim that no experiment or model run backs is marked
*argued, not run* (§N lists them). The experiments are committed in
`tests/incr-design/` (its `README` has a line per script); outputs were
in `.build/target-wp-116/wp207/`.

## M. Experiments and models (what was run, and the numbers)

**M.1 The region rule (in72 M-4, M-5).** `model_region.py`, written from
IN-72 item 2's text, over review-in72's protobuf 3.21.12 `strutil.cc`
builds and maps (the reviewer's `.build/review-in72/m/pb/`), the same six
hops. Definitions of the changed file whose address moves:

| hop | `-O0 -g`: WP-207 / WP-202 | `-O2 -ffunction-sections`: WP-207 / WP-202 |
|---|---|---|
| base | 0 / 0 of 255 | 0 / 0 of 86 |
| grow1 | **82 / 252** of 255 | **1 / 83** of 86 |
| grow2 | 80 / 252 of 255 | 0 / 83 of 86 |
| rmfn | 79 / 251 of 254 | 0 / 82 of 85 |
| addfn | 0 / 173 of 256 | 0 / 48 of 87 |
| base | 0 / 173 of 255 | 0 / 4 of 86 |

Phases: at `-O0` 153-156 sections kept per hop, one grown in place, one
overflow region (grow1, `.text`, 0x8e4c of a 0x1795e tail); at `-O2`
134-136 kept, at most 2 by first fit, one overflow region (addfn,
`.data.rel.ro`, a section in an `(O, c)` where the file had no region:
0x50 of 0x1000). No growth step. The ~80 at `-O0` are the in-section
floor of a one-`.text` object (IN-31's case). Not modelled: other files'
regions, packed sections, growth steps (none reached), a C program.

**M.2 Archive extraction (in72 B-1).** `model_extract.py`, written from
IN-71b's text (stored `KEY_REFS` rows and rounds only; the update reads
the changed files' new mentions and nothing else; a newly extracted
member is "read" when extracted), against resolve.md §4.1's closure from
scratch: R1a (weak definition after the archive, strong member before it)
and R1b (two members referring to each other, dropped together) give the
full link's member sets; 3,000 random programs (1-4 objects, 1-8 members
interleaved, 3-12 keys, strong/weak/common/undefined/weak-undefined
mentions, a root key in 30%), four hops each: **12,000 updates, 3,534
extraction flips, 0 differ.** Not modelled: listed in IN-71b. Archive
densities measured here (`nm`, GCC 14, AArch64): `libstdc++.a` 189
members, 7,271 defined globals; `libc.a` 2,022 members, 4,568.

**M.4 File-system and build-system probes.** `fsprobe.py`: on the
workspace's virtiofs and on overlay, a `pwrite` after `utime(0)` sets the
mtime to now (so an epoch mtime set before the writes does not survive
them); the lock round trip (open, `flock`, `fstat`, `stat`, unlock,
close) costs a median of 352.5 µs (p90 1,151 µs) on virtiofs and 3.5 µs
(p90 4.4 µs) on overlay, load ~20. `bsprobe.sh` (GNU Make 4.4.1, ninja
1.12.1; the "link" is `cp`): after an edit and a torn output, make
relinks for an epoch-dated or a missing output, **not for a fresh one**;
ninja relinks in all three cases.
