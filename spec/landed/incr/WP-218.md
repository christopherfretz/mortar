# WP-218 (U-6): refill, image, and the update's map

The update's image (`spec/incr.md` IN-75) and its map (IN-75, IN-79 (b),
`spec/image.md` I-78a, `spec/plumb.md` §3.2): `mortar_image::update::refill`
and `mortar_image::map::update_map`, and the decision records every
incremental-mode map now carries. Base a828a3de; seam 6 (e495d776)
cherry-picked as 78ecc9ba; the follow-up of `briefs/wp-218-rulings.md`.

## A. What refill does (IN-75)

```rust
pub fn refill(link: &Link<'_, '_>, layout: &LayoutDelta, symtab: &SymtabPlan,
              set: &RefillSet, stored: &StoredLayout<'_>, previous: &File,
              leaves: &[[u8; 32]]) -> Result<Refill, RefillError>
pub struct Refill { pub ranges: Vec<DirtyRange>, pub bytes: Vec<u8>,
                    pub leaves: Vec<[u8; 32]>, pub build_id: Option<Vec<u8>>,
                    pub counts: Counts }
```

Sorted, disjoint dirty ranges and their bytes, the NEW leaf vector (the
whole of it, for `StatePatch::leaves`) and the build id; an empty delta
returns no range and the stored leaves. In order:

0. `leaves.len() != leaf_count(previous size)` is `state-corrupt`
   (`LEAVES: …`) before anything is read or written.
1. Every input rechecked (the store's `fstat` against the file it read);
   a difference is `input-changed` (`path: error`). Again after the fill.
2. The previous section header table read from the previous output, the
   new one from the placement; a class, count or name difference is
   `update-not-implemented (image)`.
3. The moves of the growth steps (`Shift`): the sections whose address or
   offset changed, and the address map of the previous output's
   allocated sections.
4. `.eh_frame` walked (only when U-4 gave `layout.eh` or a section moved),
   BEFORE any byte is written (U9 before W1): any pointer encoding but
   0x1b/0x9b is `update-not-implemented (eh)`. A code section of a file
   whose `.eh_frame` range is not rewritten that is in `moved` is `(eh)`.
5. Moved sections' bytes copied from the previous output.
6. The image fills: the units and chunks of the set, the sections of
   `layout.files`, moved and revived sections, the holes of `hole_chunks`
   (a `DeadFragment` hole is not refilled: its bytes stay as written), the
   fragments with references (their ranges from `StoredFrag.offset/size`
   in the merged chunk), the slot chunks when `slots_appended`,
   `slots_dead` or `slots_revived` is non-empty (`slots_recounted` writes
   nothing), the rewritten `.eh_frame` ranges (or the section) and
   `.eh_frame_hdr` whole, every synthetic chunk that can hold a moved
   address when anything moved, the gaps around moved sections, the grown
   tails, and the header chunks whole. A `DeadSlot` hole of
   `layout.holes` is then written as zeros.
7. The unchanged `.eh_frame` records moved (IN-34a "pad and move"): each
   FDE's `pc_begin`, LSDA and each CIE's personality re-targeted through
   the move map, from the previous output's bytes alone (WP-239 R-3).
   WP-243's shapes: a range WITH record offsets is the image's (rewritten
   from the new records); a range with an EMPTY `records` list and a
   capacity is an unchanged range that MOVES: its bytes are read from the
   previous output at the old section offset + `stored.eh_range_of(file)
   .offset`, walked and re-targeted by `move_eh_range` (below), its FDE
   count checked against `StoredEhRange.fdes`, and put at the new range;
   a stored range missing or of another capacity is `(eh)`. A changed file
   missing from `ranges` has no records (nothing is written for it). A
   short `EhPad` (< 16) gets no `hole` line, one of 16 or more does (the
   map's hole lines, `link.rs`, as on the base).
8. `.symtab`/`.strtab` from U-5's plan (§E for the order).
9. The build id (I-76, IN-75): the blocks rehashed are those of the
   dirty runs, of the note, and on a size change the old last block to
   the new last one; each from the previous output with the writes over it
   and the note zeroed. Every other leaf is the stored one. `Leaf::kept`
   over the new vector, `digest::build_id`, the note written.
   `Counts::leaves_hashed` counts the blocks, `build_id_read` their bytes.
10. Ranges whose bytes equal the previous output's are dropped.

`pub fn move_eh_range(range, old_addr, new_addr, fdes, shift, counts)`:
one unchanged file's `.eh_frame` range moved from `old_addr` to
`new_addr` (walk, FDE count, re-target); `fdes` wrong is `state-corrupt`
(`FILES.eh_fdes`), a range that does not walk is `(eh)`.

The new output's size: `output_size(link)`; U-7 takes it as `Pair::size`.

## B. The map

- Every incremental-mode map carries the decision records of I-78a after
  the `hole`/`dead` lines. A plain map does not change by one byte
  (`i78a_plain_map_unchanged`, the static-identical gate).
- `update_map(link, layout, stored, symtab) -> Result<Map, IncrReason>`:
  the full link's map of the update's placement, `incremental`, the
  `hole .symtab`/`.strtab` lines of the plan's slack (only when the plan
  has tables), `dead .strtab <off> <size> strings`, and the `symblock` of
  every file: the plan's block for a rewritten one, `stored.symtab_block`
  for every other one, and the stored global area when the plan does not
  place it. The `slot` records of `slots_dead` are dropped (their bytes
  are a `hole`); `MapSlot` gained `index` (not rendered) for that.
- `frag`'s class (ruling 3): `<flags>,<entsize>,<align>`, flags the
  `sh_flags` bits `SHF_MERGE` (0x10) and `SHF_STRINGS` (0x20), align in
  bytes; pinned on a link by `update_map_states_the_symtab_plan`
  (`30,1,1` for `.rodata.str1.1`, `10,8,8` for `.rodata.cst8`).
- Folded sections (ruling 4, F-5): nothing folds today. The rule that
  folds is model.md §4.1 (`Folds`: a folded section takes its target's
  place); the front builds `Folds::default()` on every link, and every
  `--icf` (`all`, `safe`, `none`) is refused at the command line
  (`unsupported option: --icf`). The image and the map read
  `folds.is_folded`; the region derivation now skips a folded section
  (before, it produced a second region over its target's bytes:
  `region 2 .text 6 4212b4 c` overlapping a.o's). Test
  `a_folded_section_is_in_no_region` builds a `Folds` by hand (c.o's
  `.text` onto a.o's, given a.o's place) and shows the map: c.o's region
  gone, every other `region` line as before. When ICF lands, its folded member is in its
  target's region by place; whether `REGIONS` lists it is U-2's.
- `delta cause` lines: no rule defines them; none are written.

## C. What refill reads of an unchanged file outside the refill set (ruling 2)

The answer is NOT "none": refill's own steps read little per file, but
the image under it builds whole-link tables. No poisoning test, since
poisoning these fields breaks the fill.

| Read by | Field | Per unchanged file | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refill step 1 | `link.inputs.files[f]`, `link.recheck` | path, store handle; `fstat`, twice | IN-75 recheck: every input |
| refill steps 2, 3, 6 | `placement.chunks` (all) | its chunks' `offset`, `addr`, `size` | section tables, moves, windows |
| refill step 6 | `placement.holes` (all, filtered by `hole_chunks`) | scanned | holes to refill, `DeadSlot`s |
| refill step 6 | `link.synth` | none of its own | synthetic chunks |
| image `with_image`: `LinkTables::new` | `link.symbols` / resolution | every symbol it defines | symbol values for relocation |
| image `layout_plan::from_layout` | `placement.place[s]`, resolution row, liveness, `folds`, input section header, relocation table | every placed section | the image's plan is whole |
| image: merged, `debug_holes`, `file_fixups`, eh plan | merged frags, debug holes, fixups, `EhLayout` | all | whole-link tables |
| image fill of a dirty window | section bytes and relocations | only its sections that lie in a filled window | a window is filled whole (F-3) |

So the lazy-placement surface is: `inputs.files` + recheck (O(files)),
the chunk and hole tables (O(chunks + holes)), and the image's table
builders (O(sections + symbols)), the last being the one to cut.
`stored` is read by refill for ONE thing: `eh_range_of(file)` of a
moving unchanged `.eh_frame` range (its old offset and FDE count are not
in the placement).

## D. Seam 6 and the verifier on a `DeadSlot` hole

`a_retired_slot_is_zero_and_a_hole` (both 64-bit targets): a hand-made
`slots_dead` for `d`'s GOT slot, its `DeadSlot` hole in the placement.
The slot is zero; the update equals the replayed full link with the slot
zeroed (the note aside); the map has `hole .got 0 8` and no
`slot got <d's index>`.

`cargo xtask plumb want --map want.map --map-format mortar` on that output
and map (the map's `arg` lines spliced in from a CLI link, §F):
- x86-64: `findings: 0` (`decision records: 35 read; region, symblock
  judged (place only); …`).
- AArch64: `findings: 2`, both R4 on a.o: `the GOT-indirect reference
  reaches no slot that holds S+A (0x4613e0); R_AARCH64_ADR_GOT_PAGE
  against 'd' at 0x4212b4: expected 0x4613e0, found 0x0` and the same for
  `R_AARCH64_LD64_GOT_LO12_NC` at 0x4212b8.
- No finding names the `hole` inside `.got`: the verifier accepts it. The
  R4 findings are right: the hand-made delta keeps a.o (and its GOT
  reference) unchanged while retiring the slot, because the layout rule
  `Slots` refuses the replayed P_U with a.o's demand removed ("a got slot
  without a demand", `rows: [Symbol(Global(1))]`). On x86-64 the
  reference is relaxed, so nothing reads the slot. A real retired slot has
  no reference; the map was not shaped.

## E. How refill applies `SymtabPlan` (item 7; the resume's rulings 1 to 3)

`write_symtab` applies the plan in this order, each later step over the
earlier ones (indices are of the NEW table, a move's `from` of the old):
0. A plan that names one index twice, two `moves` with one `to` or a
   `moves` `to` that `entries` also writes, is `state-corrupt`
   (`SYMTAB: the plan writes .symtab index <i> twice …`). U-5 drops a move
   whose `to` `entries` writes (a `Changed` global a removal moves), so
   the case does not arise from U-5; refused rather than letting
   `entries` win, because the plan then contradicts itself.
1. `moves (from, to)`: every move is READ from the previous output (its
   descriptor, never what refill has written), so an appended entry that
   takes a move's `from` cannot change what the move copies. The entry
   is put at `to` as read.
2. `remap [lo, hi)` is over the table AS IT STANDS AFTER THE MOVES (U-5:
   "U-6 copies the moves, then remaps"; IN-49a at the merge): at index i
   the source is the previous output's entry at `from` when a move has
   `to == i`, else its entry at i. `st_value` is mapped through the
   growth steps (not for `SHN_UNDEF`, `shndx >= 0xff00`, `STT_TLS`);
   `st_name` and the rest as read. So a moved entry IS remapped, and a
   remap range may cover the global area. (Before the resume the range
   was read from the previous output at i with the overlay's writes laid
   over it, which put the moved entry at `to` too but would also have
   remapped anything else written there; the old wording of this section
   said "the SAME indices", which was wrong for a `to`.)
3. `fillers [lo, hi)`: over moves and remaps.
4. `entries`: over everything before. Any order of `entries` is
   accepted. An `shndx` >= 0xff00 other than `SHN_ABS`/`SHN_COMMON` is
   `update-not-implemented (image)`.
5. A shrinking table (U-5's Q2-5; the plan has no field for it): the
   bytes [new `sh_size`, old `sh_size`) after the table are zeros. The old
   end is the previous output's `.symtab` section header (its `sh_size`,
   read from the previous output with the rest of its header table; no
   stored row), the new one the placement's. If that range reaches
   another in-file section of the update it is `state-corrupt`
   (`SYMTAB: the previous .symtab's end … reaches another section …`),
   never written.
6. `strtab` bytes; `strtab_dead` is only the map's.
The tables' header fields come from `tables` through the placement.

Tests (`crates/mortar_image/src/update.rs`, hand-made tables):
`symtab_move_inside_remap_is_remapped_and_shrink_zeroed` (R removed, M
moved 4 -> 3 inside remap [1, 4), `.data` moved by 0x80: index 3 is M
with the mapped value, R's name nowhere, index 4 zero);
`symtab_appended_entry_over_a_move_source` (an entry at a move's `from`;
a move and an entry at one `to` refused). Linked
(`crates/mortar/tests/update_image.rs`, both 64-bit targets):
`symtab_shrink_onto_another_section_is_refused`. A replayed layout cannot
shrink `.symtab` on this base (the front: "a synthetic plan made from the
layout's tables is not its chunk's size"), and a fresh incremental-mode
full link has NO slack after `.symtab` (`.strtab` follows it: AArch64
201848..202112 to 201848..202088, `.strtab` from 202088), so the zeroing
is refused there. A link whose table shrinks inside kept slack, compared
with a full link, needs the join's placement (finding F-11).

## F. Findings (for the supervisor's ruling)

- **F-8 the `Slots` rule refuses a retired slot's P_U.** A replayed
  layout keeping the slot row without its demand fails `Layout::validate`
  (`Slots`). The join must either drop the rule for `slots_dead` rows or
  validate before marking them dead.
- **F-9 `update_map` writes no `arg` records.** Neither `map_of` nor
  `update_map` does (the CLI adds them); the verifier refuses a map
  without them (`no 'arg' record … spec/plumb.md §3.2a`). The join must
  add the update's command line.
- **F-10 the moving-range path** (an empty `records` list) is exercised
  by unit tests on real bytes (`eh_frame_moved_from_its_own_bytes`:
  move by 0x40, FDE still names its code; `fdes + 1` is `state-corrupt`;
  a range cut from its CIE is `(eh)`), not by a linked update: WP-243's
  producer is not on this base.
- **F-11 a shrinking `.symtab` has no linked fixture.** Replaying the
  first link's layout after a global is removed fails in the front
  (`InternalError`: "a synthetic plan made from the layout's tables is
  not its chunk's size"), and a fresh full link keeps no slack after the
  table. The join's `P_U` must keep `.symtab`'s place and capacity with
  the plan's smaller size; until then the zeroing is shown on hand-made
  tables only.
- F-3, F-6, F-7 as accepted (F-6 `.eh_frame_hdr` filled whole).
