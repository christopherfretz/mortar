# WP-255g: the pre-region padding as a `Fill` hole (S2-1); `CHUNKS.members` (VERSION 12)

Status: S2-1 (255g) and the `CHUNKS.members` column with its carried count (255g2) are built. Wiring `check_member_counts` in the update's real path is WP-255e4's (ruling "After 255g2's hand-back" item 4).

## S2-1 (ruling "After 255e2's hand-back" item 1, option (b))

`delta.rs` `Update::region_pads` is called by `holes()` once for each touched chunk, on the chunk's final hole list. For a touched region at `start > 0` it looks at `e`, the end of the last hole ending at or before `start`. It records `[e, start)` as a `Fill` hole when all of these hold:
- `e < start`, and no hole crosses `start`;
- the item at `start` does not explain the gap: either no member with bytes starts there, or `align_up(e, its alignment) != start`;
- `start - e` is less than the chunk's alignment.

**Why `e` is the previous region's end.** `REGIONS` is file-keyed, so the delta does not read the neighbour's row. Invariant I states: in a hole-tolerant body chunk, every region with bytes that is followed by another region ends at a `Fill` hole that ends exactly at its end, or else a pad starts there.
- Given I, a live byte in `[e, start)` would have to belong to an earlier region with no hole ending between that byte and `start`. I excludes that region.
- A full link's state satisfies I. A region followed by another region in its part has `Slack::region`'s whole slack, at least 64 bytes. Only a part's last region is clipped (`chunk.rs` `close`).
- `region_pads` keeps I true. It refuses `update-not-implemented (layout)` in two cases:
  - a touched region with bytes, followed by something, that has no such hole (a section fitted exactly to the region's end);
  - a chunk grown by an overflow region with no hole ending or starting at its old size.
- Without I, the hole before `start` may end at a live member's start, and a pad would overwrite that member. I is the reason a pad is never a guess.

**The capture.** `regions_check` (WP-264) now also accepts a `Fill` hole from one region's end to the next region's start. The re-capture of an update's state (front 2's reference) sees the delta's pad, and the overflow pad `overflow` already made. Without this change both are refused as "no region's slack".

**Readers of the delta's holes.**
- The map's `region` lines only attach a hole that starts at a run's last member's end, and its dead lines skip `Fill` holes. No map line changes.
- `materialize`, the refill (the made holes are filled with the section's pattern) and the capture's `HOLES` take the pad as a plain `Fill` hole.

**Evidence.** `update_two_front` x86-64 hop 2 on the 255e2 history, with hop 1 a full link (a temporary edit, reverted):
- The clip plan gives `equal (2 ranges, 987 bytes)`, where before it refused `clip byte 0x434fb written by no row`.
- The bytes at file offset 0x434fb..0x43500 are `cc cc cc cc cc` in the previous output and in the new one.

## `CHUNKS.members`, VERSION 12 (255g2)

- **The column.** `format::ChunkRow.members: u32`; format 4's row is unchanged (writes 72 bytes, reads 0). State 5's `ChunkRow` record is 76 bytes, `members` after `shndx`; a count on a non-`Body` chunk is refused (`state-corrupt`). `state5::VERSION` is 12; a VERSION 11 state is refused `state-version` like every older one.
- **The source** (ruling "After 255g's hand-back" item 3). `layout_plan::member_counts(&Model)` counts, per chunk, the sections `is_member` and `member_front(..).is_some()`: the same pair `from_layout` uses for its `Input` rows. `with_image` runs it on THE model it builds for the plan, only with `LinkOptions::keep_leaves` (incremental mode), and `write_link` carries it in `Written.members`. An update's hand-made `Written` has `None`; its re-capture counts with the same function on a `Model` of its front (the only second `Model`, on the update path only).
- **The carried count.** `LayoutRows::chunk_members()` (default not given); `LayoutDelta.members` and `members_of(ChunkId)`. `delta::layout` starts from the stored column and, per change, takes 1 for its stored row if that was a member and adds 1 if it is now placed as one; orphans take 1. A `Fate::Debug` change makes its old chunk and every non-alloc chunk unknown (`None`). A count below 0 is `state-corrupt`.
