# WP-174: kiln's kind 2 rows (K-3a, K-3c) meet mortar

Landed record. The rules are `spec/odr.md` O-4 to O-16 (kind 2), O-11,
O-32, O-35, O-47 and §9.1 (as amended here), and kiln's
`spec/abi.md` "Definition hash" (K-3c). This is the first package that
links kiln's kind 2 rows. This file gives the environment (§1), what
landed (§2), the producers table (§3), the link tests (§4), the legal
corpus under `--odr=strict` with every finding classed (§5), the cost
(§6), the list for kiln (§7), the open rulings (§8), what depends on the
new kiln (§9) and what was not verified (§10).

## 1. Environment

The host of `WP-070.md` §1 (aarch64, 12 cores shared with other agents;
GCC 14.2, binutils 2.44, LLVM 22). kiln++ is
`.build/target-kiln-k3c/release/kiln++`, built from kiln **4b017b227**
(K-3c: kind 2 rows of scheme `kiln-odr-1.d2`, anchored only; `width` in
kind 4). hod at `.build/target-hod-next`. mortar's base: `int/batch-10`
(3ad9867a). x86-64 programs run under qemu-user; i386 is not covered.

What kiln K-3c writes, as read back here (the rules the tests and the
corpus rest on):

- `odr.scheme=kiln-odr-1.d2` only on units with kind 2 rows;
  `odr.layout-scheme=kiln-odr-1` only on units with kind 3 rows (none
  at `min`). Kind 2 rows from level `min` up.
- Rows are anchored only (name 0; named rows come with K-3f). A
  definition the optimizer dropped leaves an UNANCHORED row: counted
  (`unanchored rows`), never compared, also under strict. kiln never
  sets INLINED_ONLY.
- PARTIAL (0x20): lambdas and default arguments on a definition's own
  parameters are hashed as fixed tokens until K-3e: a difference only
  there is a silent miss; a PARTIAL row that differs elsewhere is a
  warning. A lambda in a declarator (a default argument included) has no
  row (X15).
- A callee is hashed by its mangled name (Q1 as ruled; kiln's
  "Names and types"); a written `constexpr` is in the declarator.
- The hod route adds its own unit (producer=2, `reloc-model`, no
  `odr.scheme`).
- Two builds of one source hash alike: `-O0` against `-O2`, llc against
  hod, levels `min`, `default`, `detail`, `all`, and another include
  order (`kiln-k2-equal.test`, the 14-unit program's alternating units).
  No unit was found whose two builds hash differently.

## 8. Open rulings

- **Q6** (the operator's): a USER row whose own tokens are equal but
  whose SYSTEM callee is another entity at the other -std (q6) is an
  error today; pinned by `kiln-k2-mixed-std-user.test`. Rows kiln
  writes today cannot tell q6 from usertok: kind 5 rows do not list a
  definition's callees, and an object's undefined symbols cannot be
  attributed to one definition. The supervisor's direction (a second,
  "user view" hash per row, SYSTEM entities named without their
  signature) is the cheapest sound way seen.
- **libstd / libstd-type**: O-32 cannot apply to a group of a user unit
  and a libc++.a member (the library's rows are not SYSTEM); at c++17
  against a c++23 library they refuse 117 of 203 links under strict.
- **k14impl**: (a) an exclusion for the implementation's own stream
  objects; (b) an N row "same size, a union wrapper of the declared
  type"; (c) no special case, handled by a general "every row of the
  group belongs to the implementation" class (once libc++'s units can be
  marked), a warning under strict. libstdc++ does the same on purpose
  (`globals_io.cc`: the streams are aligned char arrays of
  `sizeof(ostream)`), which would show the day a libstdc++ is built with
  containers.
- **W4 §8.1** (`greeting()::g`): as WP-134 §9.

## 9. What depends on the new kiln

With `.build/target-kiln-next` (kiln d854b23bc, no kind 2 rows, no
`width`), which passes the `requires: kiln` probe, the odr lane gives
629 PASS, 12 FAIL: `kiln-k2-differ`, `kiln-k2-equal`, `kiln-k2-schemes`,
`kiln-k2-mixed-std-user` and `kiln-k2-mixed-std-user-silent` (no kind 2
rows: no finding, other counts) and `kiln-k4-shapes` (no `width`), each
on aarch64 and x86-64. `kiln-k2-silent` and `kiln-define-same-layout`
pass with either kiln. The kiln columns of `tests/mason/producers.tsv`
are recorded with 4b017b227 (the 62 cells of §3 are its kind 2 rows).
The probe cannot tell the two kilns apart: a probe for kind 2 rows
would turn the six FAILs into SKIPs (not done: the brief names the
K-3c kiln for every gate).
