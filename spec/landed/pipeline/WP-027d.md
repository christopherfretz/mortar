## BTI thunks (a64.md §9)

GNU ld and lld write a `bti c; b T` pad veneer for a target without a
landing pad. mortar now writes its `adrp; add; br x16` thunk when the
target word is a landing pad, and still refuses the target by name when
it is not. The word is read from the input. It must lie in an executable
section, inside a `Proof::Insn` run, and no relocation field may overlap
it. A `Plt` target's word is the first word `write_plt` writes. The host
enforces BTI: `br x16` to a `nop` is SIGILL. Each of `bti c`, `bti j`,
`bti jc`, `paciasp` and `pacibsp` exits 42 under mortar and GNU ld
(Q15). A `nop` target, and a `.word 0xd503245f` in a `$d` range, are
refused. A far call to an IFUNC goes through a thunk to its BTI IPLT
entry, a line GNU ld cannot link ("relocation truncated"). Tests:
`pipeline.rs` `a_bti_*`.

## `SHF_COMPRESSED` (sizing)

The references decompress `SHF_COMPRESSED` inputs and write them plain.
GNU ld and lld support zlib and zstd; mold supports zlib and zstd. The
work:
- a safe-Rust inflate (RFC 1950/1951, about 600 lines with tests) and a
  zstd decoder (RFC 8878, about 2000 lines). There are no third-party
  crates, and each needs a fuzz target.
- `mortar_input`: decompress once per section into an owned buffer, keyed
  like the parts view. The size is `ch_size`, the alignment
  `ch_addralign`.
- `mortar_resolve`: classify by the decompressed size, and stop refusing
  at `sections.rs`.
- `mortar_merge` and `mortar_image`: read the owned bytes; fill copies
  from them rather than from the file.
- `mortar_plumb`: an independent inflate, because the verifier must not
  share code with the linker.
- Writing `--compress-debug-sections` is separate (deflate).

Estimate: two packages, one for inflate plus the plumbing and one for
zstd.

## Link-line histogram

The census covers 7 drivers (gcc, g++, clang, clang++, gfortran, and the
x86_64 and i686 cross gcc). Each has 6 modes and 27 flag sets that build
systems add, giving 1134 lines from `-###`, each run through mortar. Only
option refusals are counted.
- static: every bare line links for every driver. 91 of 189 lines have

  `--version-script`, `--no-undefined`, `-rpath`,
  `--compress-debug-sections`, `--gdb-index`, `-z defs`,

  `--pop-state` (81: the gcc drivers' libgcc_s `--as-needed`).
- `-static-pie`: `--no-dynamic-linker`, `-pie` and `-z text` on every
  line.
- `-shared`: `-shared` on every line.

## Findings in other crates and specs

- `mortar_plumb/src/ifunc.rs:313-321` and `:466` (`a64_entry`): an
  AArch64 IPLT entry is found by its `ADRP`, so a BTI entry (`bti c`
  first) is taken to start 4 bytes late. Every branch to a BTI IPLT entry
  is F4, and a veneer to one is an Overflow. Not fixed; two tests assert
  exactly this signature.
- `mortar_plumb` B1 (`spec/plumb.md` §6.7): a `--build-id=0xHEX` of 8, 20
  or 32 bytes is judged as mortar's hash, because the map does not say
  the id was given. Not fixed; the linktest uses 16 bytes.
- `mortar_plumb` S11: GNU ld, lld and mold write a 6-byte build-id
  descriptor unpadded (`p_filesz` 22). mortar pads it to 24, and S11
  flags the references.
- `mortar_plumb/src/a64.rs` (veneers): a thunk's BTI target is not checked
  to be a landing pad.
- image.md I-47 (`mortar_arch/src/dead.rs`, `tombstone`): all ones in a
  DWARF 5 `DW_RLE_start_length` or `startx_length` wraps. gdb 16 rejects
  that CU's range list ("inverted range"). With g++ `-O0 -gdwarf-5`,
  `next` then prints "pc ... in read in CU, but not in symtab". Not
  fixed; a self-checking xfail is in `debuggers.rs`.
- `mortar_input/tests/s11_staged.rs:405` (`s11_4_rename_between_stages`)
  fails about 4 runs in 5 under `/tmp` (overlayfs) and passes on the
  workspace volume.

## Follow-ups

- `.comment` (S-100/R-M6). A synthetic chunk would duplicate the inputs'
  `.comment` output section. It needs either layout attaching a synthetic
  chunk to an input-named output section, or R-M6's internal-file merge
  piece, which needs fill to copy from a non-file source.
- `-Map` text (I-120 to I-122) and the switch of every consumer to
  `--mortar-map`.
- A BTI pad veneer (`bti c; b T`) for padless targets. It needs layout.
- Static-mode rows the census found, which are cheap: `--no-undefined`
  and `-z defs` (the default for an executable), and `-rpath`,
  `--enable-new-dtags`, `-Bsymbolic` and `--export-dynamic`, which have
  no effect without a `.dynamic`. These could be justified no-ops with
  evidence. `--dependency-file` needs a Make depfile writer, and
  `--warn-common` resolve's warning.
- `-z start-stop-visibility=protected` could become partial.
- `DeadRef::DeadFde` is a new variant: an exhaustive `match` in a later
  architecture crate (WP-029b) must add it.
