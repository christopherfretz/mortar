# WP-012 — the bench harness

`spec/harness.md` "bench" written; `cargo xtask bench`; `bench/`; the
first baseline of this machine and `docs/status/bench.md`.

## What landed

- `xtask/src/bench.rs` (the command: options, orchestration, exit
  statuses) and `xtask/src/bench/`: `manifest` (the workload manifest),
  `prepare` (building from pinned sources, the recording `ld`, the
  response file, the census, the stamp, the cache lock), `response`
  (capture records, response files, dependency files), `synth` (the
  generated workloads), `measure` (linkers, warm-up and rounds,
  calibration, row statuses), `record` (the results and baseline file),
  `stats` (the gate), `report` (the table and the status page).
- `bench/workloads/manifest` and `bench/workloads/src/hello.{c,cc}`: four
  small workloads, two medium (protobuf 3.21.12's `protoc`, with and
  without `-g`), `rust-self` (mortar at a pinned commit, as rustc links
  it), five generated extremes, the calibration workload, and eight
  planned rows (libc++ test, large C++ program with and without `-g`,
  kiln+hod objects, three incremental cases, the x86 rows).
- `bench/baselines/<machine id>.tsv` and `.log`, `docs/status/bench.md`.
- `Status::Killed`: a row killed by a limit is its own state, loud, with
  no figures, exit status 3; for mortar a killed link where the baseline
  linked is `LOST`.

## Decisions taken here

- **mold is measured with `--no-fork`.** By default its parent exits
  while the forked child it does not wait for holds the memory: GNU
  `time` reported 8.6 MB for a link whose peak is 65 MB.
- **Load average is recorded, not a noise criterion.** On this shared
  machine it sits near the number of processors with nothing
  interfering; the calibration link before and after measures the
  interference itself. The pool cannot be seen idle without taking its
  tokens, so the calibration stands for `docs/VALIDATION.md`'s "pool
  idle".
- **No extra tokens for a linker's threads** (the pool's refill would
  mint them); one measured child at a time.
- **rust-self is pinned to a commit** (`git archive`), so that its inputs
  do not change with every commit of mortar. rustc keeps `symbols.o`
  under `deps/rustcXXXXXX/` (not `TMPDIR`): `-C save-temps` keeps it, and
  that name is normalized out of the hash with gcc's `ccXXXXXX.res`.
- **Sources are compiled inside the cache** under relative names, so that
  `-g` objects record the cache's directory and the input hash is the
  same from every checkout.
- **16 GiB, 10 minutes** per measured link: lld at its default threads
  aborts under 1 GiB (it cannot start its threads).
- One workload per size: the generated workloads have fixed parameters in
  the manifest (the inherited draft's `--size` was dropped).
- The drafts' `exceeds` overflowed at the extremes and `calibration_differs`
  rounded a difference just above 10% down to 10%: both now compare
  exactly in 128 bits.
