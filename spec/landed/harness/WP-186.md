
readings"): commons by key; a place no piece holds, inside a section,

## The lane (xtask)

- L3 judges a `link` step's `ET_REL` output by stage R
  (`engine::verify`, when `mortar_plumb::STAGE_R`).
- `partial mortar <args>`: `mortar -m <emulation> -r
  --mortar-map=.linktest-partial-<line>.map <args>`; a refusal fails
  the unit; LT-R4 (mode 0644 read, a second link compared byte for
  byte); stage R on the output, a finding fails the unit.
- `cargo xtask plumb --compose <D> <D.map> <V> <V.map> <R> <R.map>
  --map-format F [--r-format F] [--line-d|-v|-r <file>]`: P-P1 by hand.
  A plain `cargo xtask plumb` on an `ET_REL` output runs stage R.
- Not reached: the lane adding LT-R1's two finals and LT-R2's direct
  form itself, P-P1 per final in the lane, the `skip(mold)` reasons'
  check, LT-R5, stage R on a reference's `partial` output.

## The census's `-r` class

Thirteen variations (`r-plain` ... `r-fuse-mold`, record WP-154 §A.2;
`r-plain` is in `--quick`); `OUT_OF_M4R` = `unsupported option: -r`
(until RL-7). The summary line: `census: <n> lines (<r> -r), <d>
drivers, <p> pass (<rp> -r), <f> fail (<rf> -r), in <s> s`. A `-r` line
mortar links (after RL-7) is judged by stage R and final-linked by the
driver (GNU ld) from both `-r` outputs, the two programs compared.
