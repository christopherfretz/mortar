# WP-027b — the joins inside `mortar_image`

Connects the image phase to the rest of the pipeline. Synthetic chunks
go through `mortar_synth`'s traits. Records are read from the scan's
index and the input's own RELA tables. Merged sections are filled by
`Order::frags_in`. Headers are built from `Placement` (I-40). The phase
ends with `Store::recheck`. The entry point is `link::write`
(`crates/mortar_image/src/link.rs`), checked on a real static AArch64
program that runs, is read back by readelf, llvm-readelf, eu-elflint and
`mortar_plumb`, and matches GNU ld on the header, the `.symtab` multiset
and the frames.

`mortar_image` does not depend on `mortar_scan`. `RecIndex` and `Order`
moved to `mortar_core::records` (ruling i), and `mortar_scan` re-exports
them. The image depends on `mortar_merge` only for `Order::frags_in`
(ruling ii). Synthetic chunks are held as `&(dyn SynthChunk + Sync)`,
with synth's own `FillCx` built per chunk (ruling iii).

## What WP-027a must provide

`tests/support/front.rs` restates the driver's phases on main's APIs.
WP-027a's `crates/mortar/src/front.rs` must give the same things:

1. **The assignment after `place`**, the one `Layout::validate` checked.
   The test front runs `place(..., None, ...)`, with no range extension.
2. **`records`**: the scan's `Scanned::order`, built with `RecIndex::new`.
   It must include every section whose records contain a type in
   `Facts.seq_leads`.
3. **`facts`**: a `KeyFacts` over the scan's own view (`Walker`), so fill
   sees the target kind the scan chose. The test's `Facts` is a
   simplified static version.
4. **`synth.chunks`**: every synthetic chunk that exists, in S-3 order.
   Its plans must be re-planned after layout's fixed point. This includes
   the shstrtab/shndx fixed point; the test front tries at most 3 times.
5. **`eh`**: the `EhPlan` and its `EhSource` (`MergeEh`), present
   whenever `.eh_frame` is kept.
6. **`recheck`**: `Store::recheck` on the blob holding the file. The
   model's `inputs::Blob` has no public way back to the store's `Blob`,
   so the test front uses `store.blob_of_inode(dev, ino)` through
   `output::identity(cwd.join(path))`. `ReadError::Changed` maps to
   `CopyError::Changed`, and `Os { errno }` maps to `Read(errno)`. A
   direct handle is a WP-027c follow-up.
7. **`proof`**: the scan's `CodeProof` runs. The test front builds them
   from mapping symbols.
8. **`CoreSyms`**: `__rela_iplt_*`, `__ehdr_start` and the rest are
   defined in `Placement.defined`. The test front restates `CoreSyms`,
   `LinkSections`, `A64Proof`, `slots_of`, `property_plan` and
   `synth_chunks` from 027a's front; making them public in their crates
   would remove the copies.

## Validation

All in `crates/mortar_image/tests/link.rs`. Under
`MORTAR_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1` these tests need gcc, ld.bfd, readelf,
llvm-readelf and eu-elflint.

- **The program** (`i40_static_program_runs_and_reads_back`). `_start`:
  - applies its own IRELATIVE records;
  - writes `"hello\n"` from a merged string;
  - reads two data words through the GOT;
  - calls an IFUNC and a function in another object;
  - exits 42.

  The link is `-static --build-id=sha1 --eh-frame-hdr`, and `big.o`
  (4000 `-g -O1` functions) is included. The program runs. readelf and
  llvm-readelf accept it. eu-elflint and plumb are clean apart from the
  known findings below.
- **GNU ld** (`i40_compares_with_gnu_ld`). The same objects are linked
  by ld.bfd.
  - ELF header lines: equal except Magic and OS/ABI. Ident bytes are
    equal except `[7]`, which is (mortar 0, GNU 3, a64.md Q11).
  - `e_entry` equals `_start` in both.
  - `.symtab` as a multiset of (name, type, size, visibility, section
    name, offset in section): equal after removing GNU's STT_FILE,
    section symbols and unreferenced linker-defined symbols.
    `__rela_iplt_*` is compared without its binding.
  - `readelf --debug-dump=frames`: CIEs and FDEs equal per function.
- **Staged equals resident**
  (`i81_staged_equals_resident_at_every_thread_count`). The runs are
  resident at 1 and 4 threads and staged at 1, 2 and 8. All are
  byte-identical, with `big.o`'s `.rela.debug_info` and `.text` above
  64 KiB.
- **I-110** (`i110_undefined_from_debug_only_is_refused_by_both_stores`).
  `.debug_info` names an undefined global. Both stores refuse the link
  with the same `undefined symbol` diagnostic.
- **Changed input** (`i65_input_changed_at_recheck_leaves_no_output`) and
  **truncated input** (`i65_input_truncated_at_recheck_leaves_no_output`).
  The input is rewritten or truncated at the recheck. The link fails with
  `input-changed` and leaves no output file.
- **No entry** (`i40_no_entry_writes_zero`):
  - `entry-not-found` is warned and `e_entry` is 0;
  - the `.note.gnu.property` (BTI and PAC) and its `PT_GNU_PROPERTY`
    are kept;
  - elflint and plumb are clean.
- **Windows and schedules**. 4 KiB, 64 KiB and 1 MiB windows and
  `check_schedules` seeds are covered by `tests/fill.rs`
  (`i33_bytes_do_not_depend_on_the_window`, `i32_bytes_do_not_depend_on_the_schedule`). The
  GCC/Clang 1 MiB / 4 KiB × thread matrix is covered by `tests/e2e.rs`.
  All three were updated for the new record rows and pass.

### Known findings, filtered narrowly in the tests

- **The plumb verifier** (`mortar_plumb` `reloc.rs` `in_section`, for
  WP-028b's owner). It resolves a section symbol's `st_value` through the
  listed pieces and only then adds A. S-17 needs the piece at
  `st_value + A`, and only mortar maps list merge pieces. There are 4014
  false `Rule::Field` findings on `.rodata.str*` in `big.o`.
- **a64.md Q11**: OSABI is always 0. With IFUNC symbols, eu-elflint then
  calls the IFUNC symbol "unknown type" and the IRELATIVE in `.rela.plt`
  "invalid type / symbol index". This is the "revisit if a tool rejects
  it" case that Q11 left open. GNU ld writes ELFOSABI_GNU.
- **`.relro_padding`**: eu-elflint reports the name of layout's NOBITS
  chunk.

### Other differences from GNU ld (by design or another crate's policy)

- There are no STT_FILE or section symbols.
- Linker-defined symbols appear only when referenced.
- `.rodata` and `.debug_str` lose SHF_MERGE (L-50) and get no tail
  merging.
- R, RX and RW are separate segments.
- `.got` holds only the slots, with no `.got.plt` header.
- `.relro_padding` is emitted.
- An empty `.bss` still gets a header.
- PT_GNU_STACK is always written.
- `.eh_frame` ends with a 4-byte terminator (S-49).

## Measurements (`examples/image_bench.rs`)

These were measured on a host shared with three other agents: aarch64,
12 cores, 17 GB (load 1–3). Each value is the median of 7 runs (5 at
2 GB). Before is `ec564dd`, the merge base; after is this branch. The
synthetic world and the output bytes are the same in both. Peak RSS is
equal before and after in every row: 224–242 MiB at 48 MB, 267–287 MiB
at 200 MB and 379 MiB at 2 GB.

**Wall time, ms (before → after)**

| Size | Store | T=1 | T=4 | T=8 |
|------|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| 48 MB | resident | 18.8 → 18.2 | 10.3 → 10.7 | 13.0 → 12.7 |
| 48 MB | staged | 33.1 → 29.6 | 12.2 → 10.8 | 13.6 → 13.4 |
| 200 MB | resident | 86.8 → 82.8 | 43.6 → 44.0 | 54.8 → 55.2 |
| 200 MB | staged | 150.7 → 128.0 | 49.1 → 42.1 | 56.9 → 54.8 |
| 2 GB | resident | 819.5 → 796.4 | 429.1 → 434.7 | 519.2 → 518.7 |
| 2 GB | staged | 1464.5 → 1219.6 | 492.2 → 384.7 | 550.7 → 536.8 |

**CPU time, ms (before → after)**

| Size | Store | T=1 | T=4 | T=8 |
|------|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| 48 MB | resident | 20 → 20 | 40 → 40 | 80 → 90 |
| 48 MB | staged | 30 → 30 | 50 → 40 | 90 → 90 |
| 200 MB | resident | 90 → 80 | 170 → 160 | 420 → 410 |
| 200 MB | staged | 150 → 120 | 190 → 170 | 430 → 420 |
| 2 GB | resident | 820 → 790 | 1720 → 1730 | 4030 → 4080 |
| 2 GB | staged | 1470 → 1220 | 1960 → 1540 | 4340 → 4210 |

- **Resident**: unchanged within noise.
- **Staged**: 12–22% faster at T=1 and T=4, most likely because records are now
  read from the input's RELA table at fill time instead of a decoded copy.
- **T=8 is slower than T=4** both before and after. Both builds show
  it equally, so it is the shared 12-core host, not this change.
- **Not comparable to older records**: their absolute times came from a
  24-core machine.

## Changes to `spec/image.md`

- **I-29**: `RecIndex` lives in `mortar_core`, with the leads bit. Record
  rows are `Rela` (read at fill time) or `Decoded`, and sites are
  snapshotted from the window.
- **I-40**: the list of header fields and their sources.
- **New invariants**:
  - **I-116**: `link::write`'s order and callbacks, and `KeyFacts`.
  - **I-117**: values in non-allocated sections are offsets from the
    output section.
  - **I-118**: the recheck callback; `input-changed` is keyed at
    (100, FileId, 0).
  - **I-119**: references into split sections, under
    S-17/18/19/22/50/52/53.
- **I-78**: the non-alloc records (`nplace`, `merged`), their ordering,
  and `.eh_frame` pieces; what the map does not yet write.

## Follow-ups (WP-027c and owners)

- **Recheck handle**: a direct `FileId → store Blob` handle for `recheck`
  (see item 6 under WP-027a).
- **`__rela_iplt_*` binding**: resolve.md §8.1 makes these local and
  hidden, as GNU's LOCAL. `CoreSyms` writes them GLOBAL DEFAULT, because
  the linker table's visibility is never merged into props.
- **Empty RW `PT_LOAD`** (layout): with no writable data, layout still
  writes an RW `PT_LOAD` of size 0 holding the empty `.data` and `.bss`,
  and eu-elflint rejects it ("loadable segment is writable but contains
  no writable sections"). The no-entry test avoids it by defining one
  word of data.
- **The plumb S-17 piece lookup** (above), for the verifier's owner.
- **OSABI** (a64.md Q11): decide on ELFOSABI_GNU when there are IFUNC
  symbols or GNU-specific types.
- **Map records**: range-extension and thunk records in the map (the test
  front has no range extension), and `.comment` as a synthetic chunk.
- **Symbol addresses**: `sym_addr` is `Option<u64>` (16 B) where I-92
  budgets 8 B; use a sentinel or a niche.
- **Scope item 7**, not started:
  - I-50/I-51 compression;
  - REL records (i386);
  - the x86-64 TLSGD reach;
  - `--gc-sections` in the joined pipeline.
