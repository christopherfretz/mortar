# WP-021b — the staged input store

`spec/image.md` I-81 and I-92, request R-13. The staged store of
`spec/input.md` §11.1 (b) is written and is now the default
(`StorePolicy::Staged`). The resident store is kept, selectable by
`StorePolicy::Resident`, by `MORTAR_STORE=resident` through
`StorePolicy::from_env`, and in the tests by `MORTAR_TEST_STORE`. A parts-view
reader is added to `mortar_elf` (`spec/elf.md` §3.0).

## What is resident, and why

The rules are in `crates/mortar_input/src/staged.rs`, and the table is in
`spec/input.md` §11.1. They are a function of the file's bytes only.

Resident:

- The ELF header (64 bytes), the section header table and `.shstrtab`.
  The readers parse them first (elf.md §3.1, §3.2).
- `SHT_SYMTAB`/`SHT_DYNSYM` with their `sh_link` section, and every
  `SHT_STRTAB`, `SHT_SYMTAB_SHNDX`, `SHT_GROUP` and `SHT_NOTE`.
  - The walk reads them for the symbol pass, the LTO check and group
    signatures (§6), and so does resolve (`mortar_resolve/src/tables.rs:191`
    symbols, `:232` groups).
  - `.note.gnu.property` is `SHT_NOTE`.
- `SHT_REL`/`SHT_RELA` whose target is `SHF_ALLOC`, whose own flags have
  `SHF_ALLOC`, or whose `sh_info` names no section. Liveness, the scan
  and fill read these (image.md I-82, R-13 1).
- `SHF_COMPRESSED` sections. `mortar_layout/src/headers.rs:79` reads the
  compression header, and over a missing header it would silently fall
  back to `sh_size`.
- `.gnu.warning*` by name. `mortar_resolve/src/tables.rs:814` reads the
  text with `unwrap_or_default`, so a missing body would silently drop
  the warning.
- `SHF_MERGE` with `sh_entsize > 0`, allocated or not, plus `.eh_frame`
  and `SHT_X86_64_UNWIND` on x86-64.
  - synth.md S-5 splits exactly these.
  - S-28 interns pieces by `(hash, bytes)`, so the bytes decide, and the
    representatives' bytes are needed until the image is written.
  - §4 parses every `.eh_frame` record.
  - Reading them through `Section::data()` keeps phase 60 free of
    `copy_to` and of its own copies.
- Every other section of at most 64 KiB (`SMALL`).
- An object or member of at most 64 KiB is resident whole.
- Archives: the magic and every member header. Special members (`/`,
  `/SYM64/`, `//`, `__.SYMDEF*`) and BSD-named members (`#1/n`, whose
  name may make them an index) are resident whole.
- A member larger than 64 KiB that is not a little-endian `ET_REL`: its
  first 64 bytes, which is all that §7.3 reads.
- Resident whole: shared objects (dynamic parts included, as the
  `SharedObject` reader takes a slice), thin archives, scripts, other
  formats, character devices, and any object whose section structure the
  rules cannot follow. Over a whole file, the reader then gives the same
  error as the resident store.

Not resident:

- Bulk `PROGBITS` above 64 KiB.
- Debug sections that are not `SHF_MERGE`.
- Relocations of non-alloc sections, whatever their size.

## The read plan

`Store::read_many` reads in batches of a quarter of the descriptor budget
(at least 16, at most 1 024 files). Each batch runs four parallel waves
(`spec/input.md` §11.9):

- **A.** Open and `fstat`.
  - A file of at most 64 KiB, or one that is neither an object nor an
    archive, is one whole range.
  - Otherwise read the first `head` bytes.
  - An object then reads its last `tail` bytes and applies the rules.
    Each structure the rules need that is outside those reads costs one
    more read.
  - An archive walks its header chain. Each miss reads a window of
    `head` + 64 bytes, which doubles up to 1 MiB while the misses follow
    each other within 128 KiB.
- **B.** Each archive member above 64 KiB that is an object reads its
  tail and applies the rules.
- **Merge.** Ranges closer than 4 KiB are merged into parts.
- **C.** Each part, in 4 MiB chunks, takes the bytes already read from
  A and B. One positioned read covers each run of missing bytes; runs
  less than 16 KiB apart share one read.
- **D.** The §11.5 checks: a read at `n` must return 0 bytes, and a
  second `fstat` must agree.
- **Insert.**
  - Whole files are closed.
  - Partial files are held while fewer than `max_open` are held.
    Otherwise they are made fully resident: read whole, checked against
    their parts, closed.

`copy_to` takes `&self`. It copies the parts' bytes and makes one
`read_exact_at` over the range from the first byte not in a part to the
last; the parts' bytes are then copied over that read. A short read is
`Changed`. A file made fully resident answers from its full copy.

Choosing `head`/`tail` (staged only, 4 threads, 5 runs each, load average
35–36 on 24 cores; before the C-wave split below):

| head / tail (KiB) | walk ms | read MB | read calls | VmHWM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 / 16 | 49.1 | 148.1 | 1 076 | 156 MB |
| **4 / 64** | **42.5** | 149.3 | 849 | 159 MB |
| 16 / 64 | 52.4 | 147.9 | 824 | 155 MB |
| 64 / 64 | 64.5 | 149.0 | 762 | 168 MB |
| 4 / 256 | 63.0 | 160.7 | 702 | 172 MB |

4 / 64 was kept (`HEAD`, `TAIL` in `store.rs`).

- Capping the window at 128 KiB instead of 1 MiB changed nothing (149.3
  MB, 851 reads).
- The 19.5 MB of over-read came from wave C. It read one span over all
  of a part's gaps, so it re-read the covered tail in the middle of the
  part. Splitting the read at covered runs of 16 KiB or more cut the
  bytes read from 149.3 to 136.2 MB, at 953 reads instead of 849.

## Ambiguities and decisions

1. **Merge sections are resident.** synth.md S-5 and S-28 split and
   intern non-alloc `SHF_MERGE` sections (`.debug_str`, 64.6 MB here) by
   their bytes. So R_s is 129.8 MB (123 MB of the archives), not I-93's
   59.9 MB. The difference is exactly `.debug_str` and its kin.
   - Making them non-resident would need phase 60 to stream them with
     `copy_to` and keep only its representatives (about 10 MB here). That
     is a follow-up for the synth package, and I-93 should be restated
     either way.
2. **Unlinking changes `st_ctime`.** An input renamed over or removed
   between the stages is therefore `input-changed`, a conservative
   answer. After loading, the store still reads the old inode, and
   `recheck` reports the change.
3. **`copy_to` ranges are not recorded for the hash.** §11.6 asked for
   every `copy_to` range to be recorded with its hash. That would hash
   every byte the image copies, on every link. The hash checks resident
   parts against the stream instead, and copied bytes rely on §11.5's
   `recheck`.
4. **`File::hash` is 0 under the staged store** unless
   `StoreConfig::hashes` is set. Nothing downstream reads it yet.
5. **BSD-named members are resident whole.** The name decides whether a
   member is an index, and the reader resolves it from the content.
6. **Only little-endian `ET_REL` gets parts.** Big-endian and other
   machine types go through the whole-file path, or the 64-byte prefix
   for members, and give the reader's own header errors.

## Join with main (a39bd5b: WP-023b, WP-026h, WP-025m)

`git merge main` merged with no text conflicts. `mortar_merge`, phase 60
and a new reader of input bytes, was checked against the staged store.

- **What `phase::read` reads.** It reads `Merge` sections (`SHF_MERGE`,
  `PROGBITS`, `entsize > 0`), `.eh_frame` by name (and
  `SHT_X86_64_UNWIND` on x86-64), and the relocation section of each
  `.eh_frame`. §11.1 keeps all three resident: the merge rule, the
  `.eh_frame` rule, and relocations whose target is `SHF_ALLOC`.
  - Merge sections that are compressed or placed whole (S-6) are placed
    without being read.
  - Section headers, names, symbols and SHNDX are resident by §11.1.
  - `copy_to` stays as the fallback for anything else, for example a
    non-alloc `.eh_frame`.
- **Fixes in `crates/mortar_merge`.**
  - A failed `copy_to` is now `What::InputChanged` (`input-changed`) or
    `What::ReadFailed(errno)` (`cannot-open-file`). It was
    `malformed-input`.
  - `OutOfRange` stays `malformed-input`, with its own text.
  - A `mortar_elf::Error::NotResident` from any reader call of the phase
    is `What::NotResident` (`internal-error`), never `malformed-input`.
    All five call sites go through `phase::elf_what`.
- **Test.** `crates/mortar_merge/tests/staged.rs`:
  - `s11_1_phase_60_reads_only_resident_bytes_under_the_staged_store`
    loads the native target's `crtbegin.o`, `libgcc_eh.a`, `libsupc++.a`
    and `libc_nonshared.a` with the staged store, plus a gcc-built object
    whose `.text`, `.rodata.str1.8`, `.eh_frame` and `.rela.eh_frame` are
    each above 64 KiB. That is 82 objects, 23 merge sections and 76
    `.eh_frame`.
  - It runs phase 60 with every section read by `phase::read`. It asserts
    that nothing was copied, that the store left bytes out, and that the
    phase's output (`Debug`) equals the run over the files' own bytes.
  - `s11_5_read_failures_are_not_malformed` covers the four diagnostics.
- **Dev-dependencies.** `mortar_merge` gains `mortar_input` and
  `mortar_cli` as dev-dependencies.
