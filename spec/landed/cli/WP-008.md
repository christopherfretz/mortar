# WP-008 — `mortar_cli`: GNU ld's command line, the option table, the census

`spec/cli.md` and `crates/mortar_cli` (depends on `mortar_diag` only, no
`unsafe`). The facade now parses its command line.

## What landed

- The grammar (§2):
  - one or two dashes, with the l/m/o rule;
  - joined, `=` and separate values, and values checked per row;
  - glibc-style abbreviations over GNU's names;
  - `-z` keywords;
  - `-m` emulations;
  - options of one target, checked when `-m` names the target;
  - response files with libiberty's quoting and a limit of 1999;
  - diagnostics keyed by argument.
- `Plan` (§3), which is plain data:
  - the output kind;
  - the inputs, each with its positional state;
  - search paths, scripts, symbols;
  - dynamic, `-z`, shaping, diagnostics, bookkeeping and plugin fields;
  - the options of one target;
  - everything else in `other`;
  - the requests.
- The table (§4): 471 rows, each with spellings, value, linkers,
  targets, group, status, Plan field and help.
  - `docs/status/options.md` is generated from it and checked by a
    golden test (`MORTAR_BLESS=1`).
  - `--help` and `-V` are generated from it too.
- Three kinds in `mortar_diag`, added under `spec/diag.md` §3.3:
  - `conflicting-options`: `-r` with `-shared` or `-pie`;
  - `response-file-limit`;
  - `unbalanced-option`: a stray `--end-group`, `--end-lib` or
    `--pop-state`.
- `crates/mortar`:
  - `main` reads `args_os`;
  - `link(&[OsString])`;
  - `invoke(prog, args)` also returns the `--color-diagnostics` choice,
    and `main` resolves `auto` with `IsTerminal` and `TERM`;
  - the diagnostic switches go into the collector's `Policy`;
  - errors are reported in argument order, then the refusals of options,
    then, for a line with no error, `Refused{Feature, "linking"}`;
  - `--version`, `--help`, `-v` and `-V` are answered (§4.3).

## Evidence

- `source scripts/env.sh && MORTAR_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1 cargo xtask ci --quick`
  prints `[ci] ok`: fmt, clippy (pedantic, `-D warnings`) and tests are
  clean.
- The census (§6) captures the link lines of 15 driver configurations:
  - gcc, g++, gfortran;
  - clang and clang++ 19 and 22;
  - the x86-64 and i686 cross gcc;
  - clang with `--target`;
  - rustc.

  Each configuration is linked in up to ten variants. Every line parses
  without an error to the expected kind, target and libraries.
- The differential test (§7) runs 84 common cases and 4 to 9 per target
  (88 to 93 in all), against the three
  `ld.bfd` builds.
- The lld and mold columns of the table were found by running
  `ld.lld-22` and `mold 2.37.1` on each spelling.

## Interface changes outside the crate

- `mortar::link` takes `&[OsString]` (it took `&[String]`).
- `mortar::invoke`, `mortar::Invocation` and `mortar::Color` are new.
- `mortar::VERSION` is `mortar_cli::VERSION`.
- `crates/mortar/tests/cli.rs`: a link that names `-o` now reports
  `unsupported option: -o` before `unsupported feature: linking`.
- `mortar_diag` has the three kinds above. `IDS` has 15 entries.

## Decisions taken here

- Unknown `-z` keywords are errors. The three reference linkers warn
  about them. The same goes for a value given to a keyword that takes
  none (§2.7).
- `--` and grouped short options are rejected (§2.5).
- Only GNU spellings abbreviate. An ambiguous prefix is rejected with
  its candidates. The prefix is resolved over the union of the three
  targets' GNU options (§2.4).
- The output is static when the line names no interpreter, not because
  of `-static`. rustc's `crt-static` line ends with `-Bdynamic` (§3.2).
- No pre-scan for `-m` (§2.8).
- An option of another target is accepted until the target is known
  (§2.9).
- A line with errors does not get the "linking" refusal (§4.3).
- `--no-fatal-warnings` and `--no-color-diagnostics` are supported, as
  the negations of supported rows.
- `-v` with inputs prints nothing while every link fails
  (`spec/diag.md` §7).

## Open questions

1. Unknown `-z` keywords: is "error" the right call for a drop-in
   linker? Build systems pass keywords that lld does not know (lld
   warns). A warning kind would need `spec/diag.md` §3.3.
2. Options of another target with no `-m`: the check has to be called by
   the input phase (WP-021), once it knows the target.
3. `-R DIR` means `-rpath` in GNU ld. Today mortar records `JustSymbols`,
   and the input phase has to apply the directory rule.
4. CMake's linker-id probe will not recognize mortar (it looks for "GNU
   ld" or "LLD"). This is harmless: CMake treats an unknown linker as
   GNU-compatible for flags. What libtool and meson look for in
   `--version` and `--help` (§5) was recalled, not checked against their
   sources.
5. mold lists `-z nodefs` and `-z start_stop_visibility` in its help but
   rejects them. They are recorded as not mold's.

## Follow-ups

- `-z max-page-size`, `common-page-size` and `stack-size` are kept as
  text. The phase that uses them must check them and refuse a bad value.
- `docs/status/options.md`: the census rows (§6.2) are the order in which
  options should become supported. `-o`, `-L` and `-l` come first.
