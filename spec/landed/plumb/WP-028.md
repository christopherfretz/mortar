# WP-028 — `mortar_plumb` stage 2, and L3 in the link tests

`spec/plumb.md` (§1, §2.2, §3.6, §4.4, §4.7, §5.3, §6.4, §6.5, §7, §9.1,
§9.2 (f)–(h), §10.2–§10.5, §11), `spec/harness.md` ("The command",
"Directives", "L3"), `crates/mortar_plumb`, `xtask/src/linktest`,
`xtask/src/plumb.rs` and `tests/link/anomalies`.
`mortar_plumb` still depends on `mortar_elf` and `mortar_diag` only, and
every crate is still `forbid(unsafe_code)`.

Delivered in the order 1, 3, 4, 6. Items 2 (IFUNC) and 5 (non-alloc
relocations) are **not done**: the coordinator moved them to a follow-up
package (see "Left"). Main was merged into the branch once, by the
coordinator (f8b5159); the run-wide defaults below were added after it.

## What landed

- **1. Static TLS** (§4.7). Every AAELF64 TLS type of a static AArch64
  executable is checked: TPREL and DTPREL from `PT_TLS`, with
  `tcb = align_up(16, p_align)`; the TLS GOT entries (a TPREL word, the
  GD/LD pairs); and the relaxations W9–W13 with their register
  constraints. Also new: R8 (the instruction does not fit the type;
  review finding 4), T1 (TLS without `PT_TLS`), and undefined weak
  PC-relative non-branch references resolving to the place (review
  finding 10).
- **3. Call frame information** (§6.4, `src/eh.rs`). The CIEs and FDEs of
  every input `.eh_frame` are parsed (augmentations z, R, P, L, S, B, G).
  Their pointer fields are resolved through the relocations, and each
  live FDE is matched to an output FDE by its initial location. The
  range, instructions, CIE contents, LSDA and personality must match.
  Rules:
  - E1: unparseable output, or a missing terminator;
  - E2: a live FDE is missing;
  - E3: an FDE differs;
  - E4: an extra FDE that is not neutralised;
  - E5: `.eh_frame_hdr` and `PT_GNU_EH_FRAME`.
- **4. The symbol table** (§6.5, `src/symbols.rs`).
  - Y2: the form of `.symtab`, `.strtab` and `.shstrtab`.
  - Y3: each global's binding, visibility, type, size and section against
    its definitions.
  - Y4: locals against the input locals of their name, with veneer
    mapping symbols excepted.
  - Y5: 26 linker-defined names and the `__start_X`/`__stop_X` pairs
    (`__ehdr_start`, the array bounds, `_GLOBAL_OFFSET_TABLE_`, the data
    ends, `etext`, `__rela_iplt_*`, `_DYNAMIC`, `_TLS_MODULE_BASE_`, ...). Every
    other linker-defined name is listed in `linker_defined_not_judged`.

  The static-pie `.dynsym`, `.hash` and `.gnu.hash` are named as stage 3.
- **6. L3** (`spec/harness.md` "L3"). Every link step of `cargo xtask
  linktest` that is to succeed writes a map (`-Map=` or `-Wl,-Map=`, or
  the test's own). The verifier judges the output: AArch64 `ET_EXEC`
  without `PT_INTERP`/`PT_DYNAMIC`. Every other output is counted by
  reason (`machine`, `pie-or-shared`, `dynamic`, `relocatable`).
  - A finding fails the step unless it is a §10.2 anomaly
    (`mortar_plumb::anomaly::classify`, now shared by the corpus tests and
    the harness) that is expected of that linker. The expectation comes
    either from a run-wide default in `tests/link/anomalies` (mold A1 A2 A3
    A9, GNU ld A10, never mortar) or from the test's own
    `#@ anomaly(<linkers>): <ids>`.
  - A declared anomaly that the unit never finds is stale, and fails as
    well.
  - A default that no judged output of its linker and architecture shows
    in a run of the whole corpus is a run error (exit 3), so the file
    cannot go stale. A filtered run only notes that it did not check.
  - The summary has the note `L3: the verifier judged N link outputs in
    T s`.
  - `--no-plumb` turns L3 off. Only the harness's fake-linker test uses
    it.
- Found by L3 and fixed in the verifier:
  - thin-archive members (§2.2, `thin_members`);
  - map files whose member has a `/` (split at the last `(`);
  - mold's `file:(.common)` rows (§3.6);
  - an undefined weak in a stripped output, which is now
    `symbol-unresolved` (§4.4 step 4; GNU ld and mold define
    `__rela_iplt_start`).

## Evidence

- `MORTAR_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1 cargo xtask ci --quick`: `[ci] ok`. The link
  tests give 95 PASS and 33 XFAIL (mortar, and mold's `resolve/common`),
  with L3 on.
- `cargo test -p mortar_plumb`: lib 21, corpus 5, eh 1, hostile 4,
  mutation 10, tls 6 (all pass). It took 63–65 s (baseline 53 s) on a
  quieter machine, and 113 s at a load average of about 35, when the
  unchanged test binaries also ran twice as slowly.
- **Corpus** (§9.1): every case by GNU ld 2.44, lld 22 and mold 2.37.1
  verifies with no finding but the recorded anomalies. On the glibc cases
  more than 1000 globals, more than 300 locals and at least 9
  linker-defined symbols are judged per link. More than 100 FDEs are
  checked on each libc link. Names that are not judged come only from
  mold (`$`-names, `__start_EHDR`/`__start_PHDR`).
- **Mutation kill rates** (§9.2):
  - (f) TLS: 516/516 instruction bits, `PT_TLS` 6/6, and at least one GOT
    word wherever a TLS record goes through one;
  - (g) CFI: CIE bytes 237/240, FDE bytes 318/318, record lengths 25/27,
    header bytes 156/156, terminator 3/3. E1–E5 each kill. The 5 that
    survive are equivalent, and the test lists them;
  - (h) symbols: Y2 60/60, Y3 184/184, Y4 179/179, Y5 78/78.
- **The review's miscompiles** (`tests/tls.rs`):
  - finding 1 is A8. GNU ld and lld relax IE to LE with the wrong
    registers, and the verifier reports R1 on both records of
    `tls-ie-regs`;
  - A7: GNU ld's LD to LE with `p_align` 64 gives R1 on 3 records, and
    the program exits 0 instead of 42;
  - finding 4 is R8 for every linker (`got-ldr-w`);
  - finding 10: an undefined weak `ADR` resolves to the place.
- **L3 on the link tests** (AArch64, `-j 8`): 119 outputs are judged, with
  18129 expected anomaly findings. The verifier's time is 55–57 s summed
  over the threads. The step takes 8.3/11.1 s without L3 and 19.7/18.7 s
  with it, so L3 adds about 10 s to the quick gate and no sampling is
  needed.

## Reference-linker anomalies found (§10.2)

- **A7 (GNU ld):** LD to LE adds 16 whatever `p_align` is.
- **A8 (GNU ld, lld):** IE to LE leaves the high half in the wrong
  register.
- **A9 (mold):** every global of a static executable is `STB_LOCAL` in
  `.symtab`.
- **A10 (GNU ld):** `__TMC_END__`, a label of an empty removed section,
  names a section that does not hold it.
- **A11 (mold):** `__bss_start` is 0 when `--gc-sections` leaves no `.bss`.
- **A3** is extended to Y5.

## Not judged (reported by name or count)

- `ifunc` and `non-alloc` records (items 2 and 5).
- Labels inside an input `.eh_frame` (`__EH_FRAME_BEGIN__`): `eh-frame`.
- The meaning of CFA instructions, which are compared byte for byte.
- mold's `$` symbols and `__start_EHDR`/`__start_PHDR`.
- Linker-defined names outside the Y5 table.
- In L3, outputs of other machines, PIE and shared objects, dynamic
  executables and `-r`.
- The static-pie dynamic symbol tables, which are stage 3.

- `mortar_plumb`: new `pub mod anomaly` (`classify`, `id`) and
  `pub use thin_members`. `Rule::EhFrame`, `Rule::SymbolTable`,
  `Via::EhFrame` and the new coverage fields.
- `xtask`:
  - `plumb::verify_link(output, map, format, base, allowed)`;
  - `linktest::engine::{Plumb, not_judged, map_option}`;
  - `Results::plumb` and `Matrix::plumb`;
  - `Args::no_plumb` and `--no-plumb`;
  - `format::Anomaly`, `Test::anomalies` and `format::parse_defaults`;
  - `Matrix::defaults`, `Plumb::{judged_by, defaults_seen, stale_defaults}`,
    `linktest::{DEFAULTS, load_defaults}`.
- `tests/link/anomalies` (new): the run-wide defaults. No test declares an
  anomaly now, since all of them meet only the defaults.
- `xtask/tests/linktest.rs` passes `--no-plumb`.
