# WP-268: P-E6 on `merged` non-allocated sections; `symblock` names (P-I7)

Verifier package (`crates/mortar_plumb`, `spec/plumb.md`). It was written
from `spec/plumb.md` §3.2, §5.6, §6.8, §6.9, `spec/image.md` I-78a,
`spec/incr.md` IN-49a, `spec/synth.md` S-74, S-79, S-86 and the two
hand-backs (G-5b item 1, M2b-5 item 1). No linker crate was read.
Base 8fce4ea1 (`int/batch-23`).

## 0. What needs a ruling

1. **A, §6.9 P-E6** (the accepted sentence, tightened): "a
   non-allocated output section with `nplace` units and no `merged`
   record is tiled by them, `dead` lines of `X`'s map and zero alignment
   padding; **a non-allocated section with `merged` records is not
   tiled**: its `dead` lines are §6.8's (P-I2's admission, P-I6's NUL
   end), its other bytes are the strings its references reach (§5.6
   judges each reference by content); no map lists its pieces one by
   one, so no map could tile it, with or without `nplace` units beside
   the records". The tightening: a section with both `nplace` units and
   `merged` records is not tiled either.
2. **B, P-I7** (new, §6.8 "Symbol blocks"), and the P-I2 place rules of
   §3.2. See §1. Item 5 (every local of the local area is in some
   block) and the shared-name list of item 3 go beyond the brief's
   example; both are stated by IN-49a and S-86 as I read them.
3. A pre-existing divergence, left as it was: the code passes over a
   `SHF_MERGE|SHF_STRINGS` non-allocated section with no map line at all,
   where §6.9 says one with no unit and no `merged` record holds `F`'s
   bytes. §6.9 now says so in a parenthesis; whether to judge it is
   yours.
4. `spec/image.md` I-78a still says the verifier checks "each `symblock`
   within `.symtab`'s local area" only. That sentence is the
   supervisor's.

## 1. B: the rules

P-I2 (§3.2, place): a `symblock` at entry 0; `<count>` above
`<capacity>`; `<first> + <capacity>` past `sh_info` (WP-222); its
`.strtab` region outside `.strtab`; a block in an output with no
`.symtab`; a second record of one `<file>` (or of `-`); two blocks that
share an entry of `[<first>, +<capacity>)` or a byte of their regions.
If any of these fires, P-I7 is not judged.

P-I7 (§6.8), every record in place:
1. the `<count>` entries from `<first>` are `STB_LOCAL` and not fillers;
2. the entries from `<first> + <count>` to `<first> + <capacity>` are
   fillers;
3. each of the `<count>` entries names a string inside its region
   (`st_name` and its NUL), or one of S-86's shared names (``, `$x`,
   `$d`, `__erratum_843419_veneer`) outside every region;
4. no other entry (entry 0 and fillers aside) has an `st_name` inside
   the region;
5. in a map that says `incremental`, every non-filler local of
   `[1, sh_info)` is inside some block's `<capacity>`.

Not judged: the sizes of `<capacity>` and `<strtab-cap>`, `<file>`,
and the order of the records.
Coverage line: `decision records: n read; region judged (place only),
symblock judged (place, names); capacity, order, slot, redirect, frag
not judged`.

  i386; GNU ld pairs with a `merged` `.debug_str`): a `dead ... strings`
  line gives 0 findings. On the old code it gives G-5b's two findings,

  gives 0 findings: **no check owns it**, since §6.9 does not judge a
