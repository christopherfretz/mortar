# WP-028e — the verifier judges i386

`spec/plumb.md` (§4.5 i386, §4.7 variant II, new §5.11, §6.2 S1/S2/S10,
§6.4 range-0 FDEs, §9.5 i386, §10.2 A2's ELF32 form, A19, i386
defaults), `crates/mortar_plumb` (new `src/x86.rs`, `src/x86rec.rs`,
`src/x86op.rs`; `tests/x86.rs`, `tests/x86/`), `tests/link/anomalies`,
`tests/link/tls/local-exec.test`. `mortar_plumb` still depends on
`mortar_elf` and `mortar_diag` only, reads no linker code and is
`forbid(unsafe_code)`.

## What was built

- **Item 0.** `src/x86op.rs`: the `ModRM`/`SIB` reading and opcode
  helpers both x86 tables share (x86.md R-12); `x64rec.rs` uses it.
- **Item 1.** `src/x86.rs` is x86.md §6.1's 21 M1 rows; `src/x86rec.rs`
  is the record path: A is the field sign-extended, X mod 2^32 (`TLS_LE`
  and `SIZE32` checked before the reduction), X86-13's two operand
  readings (disagreeing: R8), the GOT base per linker (`.got.plt`'s
  `_GLOBAL_OFFSET_TABLE_`; mold: `.got`; stripped: `.got.plt`), G1–G5,
  I1–I5, D1–D3, L1–L2, T1–T2 in the forms each linker writes (§5.11's
  table), the leads' A = 0 and T1's A = 0 of the 2026-11-15 rulings.
- **Item 2.** TLS variant II for ELF32 (§4.7).
- **Item 3.** `.rel.iplt` `IRELATIVE` (the resolver is the word); F5 on
  an IBT output's IPLT entries (`endbr32`); mortar's exact X86-48/49
  entries; GOTOFF to an IFUNC (F4: GNU ld's W5).
- **Item 4.** S1: `EI_OSABI` 0 or 3 and x86 `e_flags` 0 for every map;
  mortar's OSABI rule (3 exactly with IFUNC, UNIQUE or IRELATIVE).
  S2: an `ELFCLASS32` segment ends at or below 2^32. mortar on i386:
  `p_align` 0x1000, first `PT_LOAD` 0x08048000, `PT_GNU_STACK` align 16.
- **Item 5.** R-13 (b): two records at one `r_offset` or overlapping
  fields in an i386 REL section are R8 (`rel-overlap`: all three
  references apply both). The I-47 `ELFCLASS32` column was already
  `debug.rs`'s.
- **Item 6.** L3 judges i386: 98 PASS, 1 XFAIL (mold `common`); the full
  linktest is 373 PASS, 39 XFAIL, 0 FAIL.
- **Item 7.** `mutate.rs` is class-aware with the i386 forms and a new
  `header-ident` class; the i386 table and its misses are §9.5.
- **Item 8.** W1 R8 (all maps), W3 R4 + R1 and W4 R1 (mold), W5 F4
  (GNU ld), W10 A15, W11 R2 (GNU ld), W12 R8 (by code: gas cannot emit
  i386 RELA), W14 R8; W2 accepted (x86.md's accepted risk); W6–W9 and
  W16 are static-pie (outside S1); W13 and W15 are refusals by a linker.

## Cost

`xtask plumb` (release) on a static `-O2 -g` C++ i386 program (regex,
filesystem, format, locale, threads; 6.1 MB, 185 625 records): 0.09–0.11 s
wall and 69 MiB RSS under each of GNU ld, lld and mold's output.
