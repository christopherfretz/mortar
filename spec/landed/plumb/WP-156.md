## The rule

- **M11 (missing)**, **M12 (extra)** (§7.1). Run after the rules of the
  output's kind (`dynverify::verify_in`), every kind, once per map.
  Coverage: `input set: files judged n of m (…)`, or `not judged (why)`.

  records, exact; lower and upper bounds when a `-T` script or a
  `--defsym` expression leaves keys open, files between them not judged.

  (§3.8 rule 2); GNU ld's printed preamble reasons each checked; GNU ld's
  back-references, a `-T` script's roots under GNU ld, demangled reasons,
  `--wrap`ped reasons, and, under `--gc-sections` (lld, mold) or for a
  file with no section mold lists, a file's absence, are not judged and
  counted. `--export-dynamic-symbol` on the line: M12 not judged.

## What the specs leave unsaid

  for it (measured).
- GNU ld's position for a `-T` script's `EXTERN` and `ENTRY`.
- A thin archive member's identity and spelling (GNU ld prints the path);
  a member of a nested thin archive.
- Whether GNU ld's printed reason for a `--wrap`ped symbol names the
  redirected name.
- Whether GNU ld and lld extract for references of a `DT_NEEDED`
  closure's files.
- model.md §7.1's identity uses the member ordinal; maps give the header
  offset.
- `--defsym`'s expression grammar.
- The preamble's demangling (GNU ld prints C++ names demangled).
- lld's and mold's maps are incomplete under `--gc-sections`, and mold's
  for a file with only mergeable or `.eh_frame` sections.
