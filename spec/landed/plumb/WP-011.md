# WP-011 — `mortar_plumb` stage 1: the independent verifier

`spec/plumb.md`, `crates/mortar_plumb` (depends on `mortar_elf` and
`mortar_diag` only; `forbid(unsafe_code)`), and `cargo xtask plumb`.

## What landed

- `verify(output, inputs, map) -> Report` for `-static` non-PIE AArch64
  executables (§2). It runs one pass over the relocation records. Lookups
  go through indexed tables, the symbol cache and sorted occupied ranges;
  there are no quadratic searches.
- **Maps** (§3): readers for GNU ld, lld and mold `-Map` files. mortar's
  versioned text format (`mortar-map 1`) has a reader and a writer. Map
  resolution and the rules M1–M5.
- **Relocations** (§4, §5): the AAELF64 static table (§4.2) with fields,
  overflow and alignment checks. Symbol resolution. The rewritings W1–W8,
  a veneer emulator, GOT slots by evidence (R4), mergeable pieces by
  content (R6) and discarded targets (R5).
- **Structure and content** (§6): O1, I1, I2, S1–S12, and symbol values
  (Y1).
- **The report** (§7): findings sorted by address, coverage by kind and
  reason, and rendering through `mortar_diag`.
- `cargo xtask plumb <output> --map <file> --map-format gnu|lld|mold|mortar
  [--inputs-from <file>]` (§8): exit 0 when clean, 1 on findings, 2 on
  errors.

## Evidence

- `MORTAR_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1 cargo test -p mortar_plumb`: all tests pass, in
  about 50 s on this host, building the corpus included.
- **The corpus** (§9.1): 11 cases, each linked by GNU ld 2.44, lld 22 and
  mold 2.37.1, verify with no finding but the recorded anomalies. Every
  not-checked reason is a stage-2 reason (`tls`, `ifunc`, `eh-frame`,
  `non-alloc`). For C hello:

  | linker | records | verified | accepted | not checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | GNU ld | 12829 | 11069 | 7 | 1753 |
  | lld | 12823 | 9819 | 2213 | 791 |
  | mold | 12829 | 8880 | 2193 | 1756 |

  For C++ with exceptions:

  | linker | records | verified | accepted | not checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | GNU ld | 41145 | 34232 | 41 | 6872 |
  | lld | 41031 | 31340 | 8020 | 1671 |
  | mold | 41157 | 26448 | 7834 | 6875 |

- **Mutations** (§9.2):
  - (a) flipped field bits: 340/340 (6 places of anomaly A6 skipped);
  - (b) moved symbols: 42/42;
  - (c) every GOT word through which a record goes is found;
  - (d) veneer words: 9/9;
  - (e) every rule, S1–S12 and M1–M5, is enforced.
- **Hostile sweeps** (§9.4): truncated and mutated outputs, inputs and maps
  never panic, and every truncation is a finding.

## Interface changes outside the crate

- `xtask` gains a dependency on `mortar_plumb` (`xtask/Cargo.toml`;
  `Cargo.lock`), a `plumb` subcommand (`xtask/src/main.rs`) and
  `pub mod plumb` (`xtask/src/lib.rs`).

## Decisions taken here

- The map is required. Placement is never inferred from contents (§2.3).
- Foreign maps do not place merged pieces, so references to mergeable
  sections are checked by content (§5.6).
- GOT slots are found from the fields and checked by content. A slot must
  lie in the output's `.got` (§5.5).
- A veneer may start with `BTI` and nothing else (§5.4).
- The erratum workaround is not required (A4). The verifier checks what
  the program computes.

## Reference-linker anomalies found (§10.2)

- **A1–A3 (mold):** bad values of IFUNC and `_TLS_MODULE_BASE_` symbols.
- **A4 (mold):** it accepts `--fix-cortex-a53-843419` and does nothing.
- **A6 (GNU ld, lld, mold):** the GOT slot of a section symbol plus an
  addend (which GAS emits for `:got:` of a local symbol) holds the address
  without the addend. The program loads the wrong address.
