## The rule

- **Sources (brief item 1).** An `--as-needed` command-line shared
  object's strong undefined references are edges of M11/M12's closure
  exactly when it is a source of the last stage: a member of N as the
  verifier replays D-11 and D-12 from the line's inputs, AND named by
  the output's `DT_NEEDED`. Neither half alone: an output's entry
  nothing justifies is P-D6 and licenses nothing (mutation 4 below shows
  it); a needed library the output lacks is P-D6 and its references are
  no edges of that output's closure. One closure over the union of the
  edges (the least fixed point). Weak and versioned (`g@V`) references
  are never edges; nor are the references of a `DT_NEEDED` closure file.
- **The replay** (`inset::Stager` implements `dynmodel::Staging`;
  `Model::build` loops). Stage 1: resolve §4.1's closure with no
  as-needed object's reference as an edge; D-11 over the output's
  objects that closure makes live, with D-12's item-3 skip (a shared
  object's reference whose `first(k)` is a lazy file the stage has not
  made live gives no reason: 7a2; 7a1, the library first, still gives
  one). N accumulates over stages (row 2a). Next stage's sources: the
  as-needed members of N the output names; stop when they do not
  change, at most |C| + 1 stages. A link with no `PT_DYNAMIC` (static,
  or GNU ld's/mold's output of `r1c`, which needs nothing) has no
  source. Where the model does not run (§12.1), the as-needed references
  are followed by the upper bound only and the coverage says `D-12's
  stages not replayed`.
- **Texts.** M12 a non-source's references alone explain: "... only the
  references of an --as-needed shared object the output does not need
  reach it (dyn.md D-12: not an edge)". M11 a source's reference
  demands: "... (resolve §4.1; a source of D-12's last stage)". P-D6 or
  P-D7 of what such an M12 member needs: "... (only an object no stage
  of D-12 extracts makes it needed)". P-D6 of a kept library the output
  lacks: "... which D-11 makes needed and D-12 keeps needed (row 2a: N
  only grows; a later stage's member defines what it gave)".
- **Kept (item 4).** A library D-11 makes needed at stage 1 whose every
  key it gave there is defined in the output by a later stage's member
  stays needed; P-D6 no longer says "needed though nothing needs it" of
  it on mortar's, GNU ld's or mold's output of `-kept`. Without a stage-1
  reason the entry is still P-D6 (mutation 4).
- **D-19a (item 5).** Reasons from every stage; a member a later stage
  extracts stands at its archive's position, so a late joiner is placed
  at the first mention after the archive, a kept library at its stage-1
  mention. GNU ld's own neededness (`gnu_unbound`) reads the keys at the
  stage that made the library needed.
- **Judged per linker (item 3)**, from WP-167 §2: mortar strictly, and
  GNU ld and mold by the same rule (both agree with mortar on every cell

  - **A71**: lld drops the kept library (2a): the kept P-D6.
  Neither is accepted on mortar's, GNU ld's or mold's output

- **A26, A36 still say the right thing.** A26 (7a1 `shared-first`, lld

## What the specs leave unsaid

- D-12 fixes N's growth and the sources but not how a verifier knows
  "in N" from an output: the rule takes `DT_NEEDED` AND the replay. A
  library the replay makes needed that the output lacks (lld's 2a) is
  P-D6, and its references are not edges of that output (else lld's
  output would be judged by edges it does not have).
- The stage bound: D-12 says "until no candidate is left"; the replay
  stops when the sources do not change, at most |C| + 1 stages (N is
  monotone over a finite set).
- Item 3's skip when `first(k)` is an archive member a LATER stage makes
  live: the replay reads "live" at the stage it runs, so a key whose
  member joins later still gives its reason at the earlier stage, and
  N keeps it (row 2a's reading). The cells have no such case beyond 2a.
