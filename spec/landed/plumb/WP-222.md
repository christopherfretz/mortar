# WP-222 (V-1): the incremental-mode records of the verifier

Verifier package after WP-212 (`spec/incr.md` IN-49a, `spec/image.md`
I-78a, `spec/plumb.md` §3.2 and §6.8-§6.9 as WP-212 wrote them). It was
written from the specs and from the outputs. No linker crate was read.
Base 294101b1 (integration batch 12).

## 1. What was implemented, by item

**Item 1: §6.9 P-E3 fillers** (`src/symfill.rs` `Table`, `Form`;
`src/equiv.rs` `fillers`):

- The form. In a side whose map says `incremental`, an entry before
  `sh_info` of the form is passed over by every P-E3 rule and counted:
  `symbol table: fillers=<n>` in that side's summary, and `, fillers=<n>`
  on `xtask plumb --equiv`'s `X:`/`F:` line. A plain side's lines are
  unchanged.
- A filler in a side whose map does not say `incremental` is P-E3:
  `F: .symtab entry i is a filler, in a link whose map does not say
  incremental`.
- An entry of the form at or after `sh_info` is P-E3: `... is a filler
  at or after sh_info n`.
- A near-filler (exactly one field off) is P-E3 naming the field:
  `... is a filler but for its <field> (a near-filler: S-79, mortar
  writes no such entry)`. The fields are `st_name`, `st_info` (the type
  `STT_NOTYPE`), `st_other`, `st_shndx`, `st_value`, `st_size` and
  `.symtab_shndx`.
- The locals, with fillers excluded, are compared as a multiset (the
  `skip` sets in `globals`/`locals`).
- A side with section symbols is not judged (amendment, §3 F-3).

**Item 2: §6.8 file slack** (`symfill::room`, `symfill::slack`;
`dead.rs` `Range.slack`):

- `hole .symtab|.strtab <sh_size> <n>` in an incremental map. Its end is
  bounded by the nearest higher file offset of a section that has file
  bytes, by `e_shoff` and by the file's end, whichever comes first.
- It holds zero bytes (P-I5) and counts toward no coverage.
- `debug_ranges` and the DWARF walk do not see it.
- Any other non-alloc hole stays P-I2.

**Item 3: §6.8 `.strtab` dead names** (`dead.rs` `strings_section`,
`symfill::check` P-I3):

- `dead .strtab ... strings` is admitted in an incremental map and is
  judged by P-I6 (it ends with a NUL).
- P-I3: no entry other than a filler has its `st_name` inside such a
  range. The finding reads `.symtab entry i ('name') names .strtab+off,
  in a dead range`.
- In a plain map the range stays P-I2 (`in a section with no merged
  strings`).

**Item 4: §3.2 decision records** (`map/mod.rs` `Decisions`, `Map.decisions`;
`map/mortar.rs`; `symfill::check`):

- The reader raises every MapError the text lists:
  - field counts, with `slot` taking 2, 3 or 4 after the word, and its
    2-field form for `tls-ld` only;
  - numbers;
  - `<part>`, `<kind>`;
  - the hash's length and digits;
  - a `<file>` without its record;
  - a decision record in a map without `incremental`;
  - a decision record before `incremental`/`hole`/`dead` or after a
    dynamic record;
  - the group order.
- The writer emits them in that order.
- P-I2 is judged for:
  - a `region` whose `<out>` names no section (`names 0 output
    sections`) or several;
  - a `region` not inside `[sh_addr, sh_addr + sh_size]` (`is not inside
    '.text'`);
  - two regions of one section that meet (`meets the region of line
    n`);
  - a `symblock` with `<first> + <capacity>` above `sh_info` (`runs past
    .symtab's local area`).
- The last two are the two the brief named. Each has a one-sentence
  text, and no correct writer can trip it: IN-30 makes regions disjoint,
  and IN-49a puts a block and its reserve in the local area. They were
  implemented, so I-78a's "region judged" now lags (F-2).
- The coverage line: `decision records: n read; region, symblock judged
  (place only); capacity, order, slot, redirect, frag not judged`.

**Item 5: `common <file>`** (`CommonAlloc.file`):

- The field is read only in an incremental map.
- A fifth field in a plain map is a MapError.
- A `<file>` with no `file` record is a MapError.
- A missing field in an incremental map is a MapError only when the map
  has a decision record. This is transitional, ruled (§4 F-1, §6).
- P-E2 compares the files by identity where both maps state them:
  `the common 'n' is allocated from <file> in the update, <file> in the
  full link`.
- Against a plain F it is read, not judged, and named:
  - `equiv not judged: common <file> of n common(s): a map states none
    (resolve.md §3.3's winner is not derived)`;
  - what it would take: §4, the last item.

**Item 6: tests** (`tests/symfill.rs`, fixtures `tests/symfill/`):

- The fixtures. `gen.sh` builds them: clang `-g -fcommon -O1
  -nostdlib`, static, one program on AArch64 and on x86-64. `x-<arch>`
  is linked by mortar `--incremental=full`, and `f-<arch>` is the plain

- The editor. `edit.py` works on bytes and map lines only and imports

- The tests:
  - `in49a_shape_is_accepted`
  - `p_e3_nonalloc_locals_by_identity`
  - `near_filler_reported`
  - `p_e3_misplaced_fillers`
  - `p_i2_p_i5_file_slack`
  - `p_i3_p_i6_dead_strtab_names`
  - `p_i2_decision_records`
  - `p_e2_common_file`
  - `p_e1_p_e2_pad_relro_may_be_empty_in_one_side`
- The map unit tests: `map::mortar::tests::s3_2_decision_records_round_trip`,

## 2. L10's findings (WP-067's L-1, L-2), bytes first

**L-1** is outcome (ii), the verifier was wrong.

- The bytes: the gen200 `clang-a64-O0-g-fs-static` seed 1 base pair has
  933 P-E3 findings. All are `$d` of the NON-allocated debug sections
  (`.debug_abbrev/info/line/rnglists/str_offsets/addr`), not
  `.eh_frame`: P-E3 already passed over `.eh_frame`'s locals.
- WP-067's reading (`.eh_frame` keeps more records) is therefore not
  what the findings were.
- X's debug sections carry IN-51's slack (dead `neutral` lines), so the
  debug units sit at other offsets. The multisets of identities
  (section, unit, input offset) of the 1608 debug `$d` are equal on both
  sides.
- The verifier's error: §6.9's identities covered allocated items only,
  so a non-allocated local fell back to equal values.
- The fix: a local of a non-allocated section corresponds through that
  section's `nplace` units (§6.9, `Side::nonalloc_ids`).
- What a wrong link must still get right: a `$d` that lies in no unit,
  or in another unit's range, still corresponds to nothing (mutation
  `l1`).

**L-2** is outcome (i), the rule was too strict.

- The bytes, corpus40 `clang-x64-O2-nog-nofs-static` seed 2 step 6:
  - F has a `.pad.relro` header of size 0 at 0x535000, with no `chunk`
    record (§3.2: the record exists for a size above 0);
  - X's pad is 0x428 bytes.
- L-64 sizes the pad from the addresses, and IN-72's slack moves the
  addresses, so the two layouts are both correct.
- The amendment: P-E1's segment section lists and P-E2's synthetic chunk
  set leave `.pad.relro` out. Its header (name, type, flags) still
  compares at its index (P-E1), and each side's S20 and M6 still judge
  it.
- The test: `p_e1_p_e2_pad_relro_may_be_empty_in_one_side` uses mortar's
  dynamic `k2a` with F's pad emptied. The base verifier gives exactly
  L-2's three findings on it; after the amendment there are none. The
  pad not writable in X is P-E1.

The regression replays with this tree:

- `gen200.clang-a64-O0-g-fs-static.seed1`: PASS
- `corpus40.clang-x64-O2-nog-nofs-static.seed2`: PASS
- `gen200.clang-a64-O2-nog-nofs-static.seed1`: PASS

Each replay's summary line is `L10 SUMMARY sequences=1 failed=0
flagged=1 refused=0`. The flag is the lane's "only fell back", as
before.

## 4. Findings and questions

- **F-1 (ruled 2026-11-18: accepted, transitional; §6).** The text requires `<file>` on every `common` of an
  incremental map. Today's linker writes four fields, so the whole L10
  lane would be MapErrors.
  - What is implemented: required only in a map with a decision record.
  - Proposed text: "until the linker writes the decision records, a
    `common` without `<file>` is a `MapError` only in a map that has a
    decision record".
  - Or: make it required now and land the linker side first.
- **F-2 (ruled: accepted; the supervisor put the sentence into I-78a at
  the merge).** I-78a says the verifier judges
  "syntax and region". Proposed: "... syntax, each `region`'s place in
  its section and against the other regions of that section, and each
  `symblock` within `.symtab`'s local area".
- **F-3 (P-E3, amended; ruled: accepted; §6).** GNU ld writes an unnamed `STT_SECTION` symbol
  per section, and section 1's has exactly the filler form. A side with
  such a symbol of another section `k` at `k`'s address therefore
  follows another linker's convention: its fillers are not judged, and
  the equiv summary names this (§6.9). Without the switch, every GNU
  side would be 1 P-E3. It is not reached by mortar (S-79).
- **What the specs leave unsaid:**
  - whether the room of the file slack counts `NOBITS` sections (taken:
    no) and the file's end (taken: yes);
  - whether `<first>` of `symblock` counts from 0 or 1 (the check is
    `first + capacity <= sh_info` either way);
  - P-E2's meaning against a plain F (now written: read, not judged).
- **What judging `common <file>` against a plain F would take.**
  The verifier would need resolve.md §3.3's `≺` over the commons'
  mentions: the order of the files on the line, archive member
  extraction order, and the largest-size rule for `SHN_COMMON`. Today
  the input set (§3.8) derives only `first`/`cfirst` per key, not the
  winning common's file. It is a model of its own: one key's mentions,
  ordered by ≺, with the size and alignment rules, tested on gcc
  `-fcommon` pairs.
