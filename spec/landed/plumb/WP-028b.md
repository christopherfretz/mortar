## What landed

- **6. S7 and `e_entry = 0`** (§6.2). An entry of 0 is accepted: it is
  the gABI's "no entry point", and a link with no entry symbol writes it
  (`layout.md` L-103).
- **1. IFUNC in static outputs** (§4.8, `src/ifunc.rs`). F1: every
  `IRELATIVE` lies between `__rela_iplt_start` and `__rela_iplt_end`, and
  its addend is a resolver (an `STT_GNU_IFUNC` definition). F2: each slot
  is an aligned, writable word no other record or slot uses. F3 and F4:
  every reference to an IFUNC reaches an IPLT entry of its resolver, as a
  branch, an address or a GOT-indirect pair. The `ifunc` test case now
  applies its own `IRELATIVE` records, so the freestanding program runs.
- **2. Non-alloc relocations** (§5.8, §10.6, `src/debug.rs`). DWARF
  records are judged. Tombstones follow per-linker rules: lld and mold
  write 1 in ranges and loc sections and 0 elsewhere; GNU ld writes 1 in
  `.debug_ranges`, 0 elsewhere, and redirects to the kept copy of a
  COMDAT group. mortar must follow I-47 and I-112.
  - R-9's `nplace` and `merged` records are read, with M2 (placed twice)
    and M5 (two overlap).
  - Compressed output sections are counted as `compressed`. mold's map
    lists no input rows for them.
- **4. Program properties** (§6.6, `src/property.rs`, P1 and P2). The
  `.note.gnu.property` of the output is the merge of the inputs' notes.
  How it is merged depends on the property type and the linker:
  - merge rules: AND, OR, OR-all, max, present, equal-or-absent;
  - a per-linker table that says merged, dropped or not judged;
  - S-126 for several notes in one mortar object.

  P2 checks the section's form, `PT_GNU_PROPERTY` and `PT_NOTE`.
- **Layout rules** (§6.2, from `layout.md` §20.3, renumbered):
  - S2: `p_align ≥ 4096` under mortar's map;
  - S13: `PT_PHDR` and `PT_INTERP` are unique and placed first;
  - S14: no `NOBITS` section past `p_filesz`;
  - S15: `sh_addr` is aligned;
  - S16: no writable byte before a RELRO section in its page;
  - S17: no non-alloc section inside a `PT_LOAD` file image;
  - S18: under mortar's map, a `PT_LOAD` ends at a section end.
- **5. Requests applied:**
  - `synth.md` R-P1 (as P1, P2, F1 and F2), R-P2, R-P4, R-P5, R-P6 and
    R-P7; R-P3 in part;
  - `layout.md` §20.3, except S19 and the L-13 clause;
  - `a64.md` §8.7: the long veneer and the register `ADD` are followed;
  - `image.md` R-9 and R-8.
- **3. Build id** (§6.7, `src/buildid.rs`, B1, about 470 lines with the
  tests). SHA-1 and SHA-256 are written from FIPS 180-4, and `L128` from
  I-114's text. I-72's three trees use 1 MiB leaves, and only the leaf
  that holds the id is copied. Under mortar's map, an 8-, 20- or 32-byte
  `NT_GNU_BUILD_ID` must equal the tree's prefix. The public
  `buildid::seal` rewrites an id the way mortar would; tests use it to
  turn a reference output into a mortar one.
- **Found and fixed in the verifier:** S11 rounded an 8-aligned note's
  name size up to 8. It now aligns the descriptor's offset instead, so a
  property note's 4-byte name is not padded. This was a false S11 on
  every GNU ld property note.

## Reference-linker observations (§10.2, §10.6)

- No new anomaly id. A2 now also covers mold giving an IFUNC alias the
  value `2^32 + x` (Y1).
- Recorded as notes, since they are never findings:
  - lld ends RELRO at a 4 KiB boundary with a 64 KiB `p_align`;
  - GNU ld writes no `PT_GNU_STACK` when no input has `.note.GNU-stack`;
  - a `PT_LOAD` that is both writable and executable (L-6).
- **Tombstones:** GNU ld 2.44 redirects a reference from a dropped COMDAT
  copy to the kept copy. After `--gc-sections` it writes 1 in
  `.debug_ranges` and 0 elsewhere.
- **Properties:** GNU ld and lld AND BTI and PAC across objects, but OR
  several notes inside one object. mold writes no AArch64 property note.
- GNU ld puts the first 24 bytes of `.got.plt` inside RELRO.

## Spec contradictions found

- `image.md` §15 says GNU ld writes "the redirect or 0" in
  `.debug_ranges`. The `gc` experiment (GNU ld 2.44, `-gdwarf-4 -O2
  --gc-sections`) shows 1.
- `layout.md` §20.3's S13–S20, T1, Y2, A7 and A8 collide with ids that are
  already taken, and its S16 and S17 collide with `synth.md` R-P1's. All
  were renumbered (see the "Applied" marks).
- `synth.md` R-P1 S17 says the `IRELATIVE` slots are "a word of
  `.got.plt`". lld and mold use other sections, so F2 does not name the
  section.
- `layout.md` L-8 counts `.got.plt` as outside RELRO, but GNU ld puts its
  first 24 bytes inside. S16 accepts this.
