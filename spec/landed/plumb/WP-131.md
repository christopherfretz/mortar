## V5 — a dynamic program without `.dynamic` passed

**Cause.** `dynverify.rs` `verify_with` took "dynamic" from the output:
`DynOut::read` needs `PT_DYNAMIC`, and without it the output went to the
static `crate::verify`. There S1 accepts any `ET_EXEC`, and no rule
reads `PT_INTERP`, `.dynsym` or a `JUMP_SLOT`. mold 2.37's output after
`--push-state -Bstatic --pop-state -lgreet` (PT_INTERP, `.dynsym`, a PLT,
a JUMP_SLOT, no `.dynamic`; it crashes) was judged static and clean.

**Rule** (`spec/plumb.md` §12 P-D4, "No `PT_DYNAMIC`"): the kind comes
from the line and the inputs, never from what the output holds. With a
dynamic line (`Exec`, `Pie`, `StaticPie`), or `PT_INTERP`, an
`SHT_DYNSYM`, a `.dynamic` section, or a dynamic record other than
`IRELATIVE`, a missing `PT_DYNAMIC` is P-D4, and each shared object the
line loads (not `--as-needed`) is P-D6 (no `DT_NEEDED`). Such an output
gets the structure rules and these findings only.

**Site.** `dynverify.rs` `verify_with`, the `let Some(dout) =
DynOut::read(&image) else` branch; new `line_words` and `no_dynamic`.

**Tests.** `tests/dyn.rs` `s12_p_d4_no_pt_dynamic` (case `nodyn`: each
linker's lazy no-PIE hello with `PT_DYNAMIC` made `PT_NULL` is P-D4).
`tests/link/dyn/shlib/push-state.test`: `skip(mold)` became
`xfail(mold)` with the L3 P-D4 finding (aarch64 and x86-64).

## V1, V6 — one reader of the line

**Cause.** `line.rs` had its own spellings and its own `WITH_ARG` list:
two-dash forms of single-dash names, abbreviations and refused options
with a separate value (`--sort-section alignment`) were misread.

**Rule** (§3.2a): `opts.rs` holds every row of
`docs/status/options.md`, accepted (`ACCEPTED`) and refused (`REFUSED`),
held equal to the table; `opts::Table::word` reads one word and its
value by cli.md §2; `line.rs` dispatches on row ids
(`line::INTERPRETED`, `INTERPRETED_Z`). Tests:
`line::tests::s12_1_every_spelling_of_a_row_reads_alike` (every
spelling of every row, abbreviations and `-z` keywords, both value
forms: an interpreted row changes the line, any other leaves it equal),
`s12_1_v1_two_dashes`, `s12_1_v6_sort_section_takes_the_next_word`.
After WP-111 (`--sort-section`, `--sort-common`, `--warn-common`,
`--no-warn-common`, `--verbose` accepted) the rows moved from `REFUSED`
to `ACCEPTED`. `tests/link/layout/sort-section.test` gained trylink's
spelling (`-Wl,--sort-common -Wl,--sort-section,alignment
-Wl,--warn-common`) on a `-no-pie` and a `-pie` link (AArch64, x86-64):
no P-D2, where WP-111's record saw "the command line: alignment: cannot
be read".

## V3 — S2 reads the line's maxpage

**Cause.** `structure.rs` `check_layout`, the i386 S2 clause, held
`p_align` at 0x1000 and the base at 0x08048000.

**Rule** (§6.2 S2 (mortar)): maxpage is the line's last `-z
max-page-size` (0 or none: 64 KiB AArch64, 4 KiB x86-64 and i386); each
`PT_LOAD`'s `p_align` is max(maxpage, its sections' largest alignment)
(L-67, L-74), on every machine; an i386 `ET_EXEC` starts at
align_down(0x08048000, P₁) (L-68). `-z common-page-size` changes nothing
(L-75). The 64-bit base is not judged: the corpus tests read lld's
outputs (base 0x200000) under mortar's map. `check_layout` now takes the
`Map` (call sites in `lib.rs`, `equiv.rs`, `dynverify.rs`: one argument
each). Tests: `tests/mutation.rs` (maxpage 2 MiB on the line: S2 on
every `PT_LOAD`; `-z common-page-size=0x1000` and `-z max-page-size=0`:
none); `tests/link/layout/max-page-size.test` lost
`xfail(mortar,i386)` and gained a mortar `-z common-page-size` link
identical to `plain`.

## V2 — lld's `DF_SYMBOLIC`

**Rule** (§10.2 A44, lld only): under `-Bsymbolic` (the last of its
family, `Line::symbolic`) a reference's D-30 finding "DT_FLAGS … has bits
…" is split: `DF_SYMBOLIC` gets its own P-D6 message
(`dynverify::SYMBOLIC`), which `anomaly.rs` names A44 for lld; other
bits keep D-30's message. mortar's rule is D-30's, unchanged. Site:
`dynverify.rs` `verify_with`, after `dyncheck::check`; new
`symbolic_flag`. `tests/link/dyn/shlib/bsymbolic.test`: lld's
`-Bsymbolic` link is back, `#@ anomaly(lld): A44`.
