# WP-032a — performance: input and resolve (phases 20–30)

Branch `wp/032a-perf-input`, cut from main d7a12b9 (first light), main
78dd0f9 merged once. Crates: `mortar_input`, `mortar_elf`,
`mortar_resolve`, the phase 20–30 lines of `crates/mortar/src/front.rs`;
additive: `xtask` bench (the golden output check, item 6 of the brief).

## Method

- `MORTAR_TIME_STEPS=1` (new, always compiled; stderr only, decides
  nothing) prints every sub-step of phases 20 and 30 and the work counted:
  `mortar: time-steps: input.read.c: 45.73 ms`,
  `mortar: time-steps: input.count.globals: 107647`. Sources:
  `mortar_input::load::load_steps`, `StoreStats::wave_ns` (waves A–D of
  the staged store), `mortar_resolve::Outcome::steps`
  (`InputsView::new_steps`, `resolve_steps`).
- Inputs copied to one `/tmp` directory (equal page-cache state for every
  linker). Interleaved runs (A B A B …), min and median of 9–11; the host
  is shared and its load average was 7–13 throughout (stated per table).
- Byte identity: every commit was checked against d7a12b9's output, map
  and stderr on protoc and the four synthetic workloads, at threads 1, 8,
  8 + `MORTAR_SCHED_SEED=977`, 1 + seed, and 4 with `MORTAR_STORE=resident`
  (25 links per commit, all identical). Hashes (sha256, 16 digits):
  protoc 1c1dc7f70d2d4404, synthetic-comdat eb557a1fb002c7a1,
  synthetic-sections 220cd8e38e664c71, synthetic-archives
  159ba4f52cc5d923, synthetic-objects f974aa34308feeea.

Phase 20 stops scaling at 4 threads: waves B and C are bound by the
kernel's copy and page faults (below), wave B's parallel region is short
and its grain coarse. On synthetic-objects (3 437 small files) wave A
takes 29.7 ms at 1 thread and 27–70 ms at 8 (not the descriptor budget:
the same with `ulimit -n 65536`); kernel symbols cannot be resolved by
`perf` in this container, so the cause (path lookup on overlayfs is the
suspect) is not established.

Other shapes, 1 thread, before → after: synthetic-comdat (174 089 groups)
phase 20 121.4 → 108.0, phase 30 49.1 → 33.7 (intern COMDATs 12.9 → 2.4,
per file 24.6 → 10.2); synthetic-objects (542 507 mentions) phase 20
135.8 → 113.8, phase 30 84.6 → 21.0 (intern symbols 58.9 → 40.3);
synthetic-sections (269 419 sections) phase 20 80.1 → 63.4, phase 30
15.8 → 12.5.

What the reference linkers do differently (reasoned from `--perf` /
`--time-trace` rows and first principles, not from their source):
- reading: mold and lld `mmap` their inputs; nothing is copied and a
  page is faulted in (with fault-around, 16 pages at a time) only when
  touched. mortar copies 138.6 MB into owned buffers: the copy plus a
  fault per 4 KiB page is wave B + C, 60 ms of a 1-thread link. A
  measured floor for 117 MB with `pread` into fresh buffers is 27 ms warm
  (20 ms copy, 8 ms faults; prototype outside the crates).
- archive members: mold reads only the symbol tables of members until
  they are extracted; mortar makes every member's resident parts resident
  (30.1 MB of the 138.6 MB belong to the 2 744 dead members).
- resolution: mold's resolve row (~1 ms at 8 threads) is a lookup per
  mention in a concurrent table built while parsing. mortar's interning is
  the same kind of table; resolve's remaining cost is the per-file view
  (re-decoding every group of every file, dead ones included, because its
  faults are reported for all files) and d1.

## The golden output check in `cargo xtask bench`

mortar's warm-up outputs are hashed (FNV-1a 64); outputs that differ
between its default and one-thread configurations fail both links; the
hash is the header key `output_hash.<workload>`; `--check` prints
`OUTPUT CHANGED <workload>: old -> new (same inputs)` as information (a
performance commit keeps the hash, a feature commit may change it).
Proposed regression threshold for VALIDATION.md: on the CPU median of
mortar's one-thread row (less noisy than wall on a shared host), fail at
+10 % against the baseline with `noisy: no`, warn at +5 %; never gate on
wall time.

## `unsafe` (not written; for the operator)

| site | safe cost on protoc | estimated saving | invariant to audit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mmap` of inputs instead of the staged store's owned parts (`mortar_input/src/store.rs` waves B/C) | waves B+C: 60 ms at 1 thread (14.7 + 45.7), 41 ms at 8; 138.6 MB anonymous | 40–50 ms at 1 thread (copy and faults gone; fault-around), 20–30 ms at 8; −138 MB anonymous memory (file pages instead) | a mapped file truncated during the link raises SIGBUS (must be handled or accepted, as mold does); bytes may change under the link (the §11.6 guarantee "bytes hashed are bytes linked" becomes best effort); slices must not outlive the mapping |
| persistent worker pool instead of scoped spawn per region (`mortar_core::par`) | a region costs 90/169 µs (min/median) at 8 threads, 141/248 at 12 (prototype); phases 20–30 run ~35 regions | ~5 ms at 8 threads (~8 % of phases 20–30) | jobs borrowing the caller's stack must finish before the region returns (the lifetime erasure scoped threads give for free) |
| uninitialised part buffers (`vec![0; n]` in wave C) | none measurable: parts are large, `calloc` maps zero pages | < 1 ms | every byte written before read |

**Safe alternative found:** `GLIBC_TUNABLES=glibc.malloc.hugetlb=1`
(glibc 2.35+ makes malloc `madvise` its mappings for transparent huge
pages; THP is `madvise` on this host). protoc, median of 11 interleaved,
load 7–8: 1 thread total 576.0 → 533.3 ms (phase 20 82.1 → 58.6), 8
threads 244.8 → 216.7 ms, CPU 870 → 800 ms; peak RSS +5 MiB. No code is
unsafe; the variable must be set before the process starts: a proposal for
the driver (re-exec itself once with it when unset) or for the bench and
the operator's wrapper.

## Proposals (interface or architecture; not made)

1. **Dead members' bulk** (`spec/input.md` §11.9, `store.rs` wave C): make
   only the fact parts (headers, symbol and string tables, groups) of an
   archive member resident in phase 20, and its other resident parts
   (relocations of allocated sections, merge and `.eh_frame` sections,
   small sections) after phase 30, for live members only. protoc: −30.1 MB
   resident (−14 % of peak RSS), ~10 ms of wave C at 1 thread. Changes the
   store's contract (which ranges `resident` serves when) and needs a
   driver step after resolve.
2. **The COMDAT numbering in `Inputs`**: `load.rs` discards the per-group
   `ComdatId` map the interner computes; resolve's per-file pass hashes
   every signature again to find it. A field in `mortar_core::inputs::Inputs`
   breaks struct literals in other crates' tests, so it is left as a
   proposal (saving ~1 ms at 1 thread on protoc, ~3 on synthetic-comdat).
3. **Driver: `GLIBC_TUNABLES`** as above.

## Spec findings

- `spec/input.md` §11.5 amended: the second `fstat` also compares
  `st_nlink`. `s11_4_rename_between_stages` failed 16 of 20 runs with its
  scratch under overlayfs `/tmp` (0 of 10 under `/Volumes`): the coarse
  clock gave the write and the unlink a moment later one `st_ctime` (191
  of 200 trials); `st_nlink` drops on every such unlink (200 of 200). Not a
  hole in what is linked: the held descriptor pins the inode, whose bytes
  did not change; only the conservative diagnostic was missed. Now 0 of 30
  under `/tmp`.
- `spec/elf.md` "parsing an object costs constant time" rules out caching
  the symbol-table index at parse; every phase scans the section headers
  per file instead (now one cheap pass). A lazily filled cache would need
  interior mutability in a `Copy` view; not worth it at the measured cost.
- `spec/harness.md` header keys: `output_hash.<workload>` added.

## What remains (ranked by estimated saving, protoc)

1. `store.rs:1141-1170` wave C and `store.rs:1113-1138` wave B: the copy
   and faults (mmap: unsafe table; THP tunable: proposal 3); ~40 ms at 1
   thread.
2. Dead members' resident parts (proposal 1): ~10 ms, −30 MB.
3. `mortar_core/src/intern.rs:566-587` pass 1: 80 ns/mention, a cache miss
   per probe on a 56-byte-entry table plus the key compare in the input's
   bytes; a 16-byte entry (hash + key index) would halve the table. Not my
   crate: ~3–4 ms at 1 thread.
4. Wave A not scaling on many-file links (synthetic-objects): unexplained,
   see the profile.
5. `tables.rs:271-311` per file: every section of every file decoded to
   find its groups (the facts pass knows their indexes): ~2 ms.
6. `resolve.rs` d1: 6 ms at 1 thread, scales to 1.7 at 8: not looked at.
