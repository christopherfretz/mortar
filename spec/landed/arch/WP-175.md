# WP-175 — a `NONE` relocation overlaps nothing (landed)

Branch `wp/175-none-overlap`, cut from `int/batch-11` (12ba959e). The
finding is WP-154's: GNU ld's `-r` of two C++ units sharing an inline
function drops the losing COMDAT copy's FDE and rewrites its `.eh_frame`
record as `R_*_NONE`, whose `r_offset` can equal a live record's
(AArch64: `PREL32` and `NONE` at 0x6c). mortar refused the AArch64 file
(`overlapping relocations`); x86-64 and i386 linked.

## The fix

`crates/mortar_a64/src/table.rs` `h()`: a `Field::None` row of class
`NO` (types 0 and 256) gets the window `(0, 0)`; the TLSDESC markers
567-569 keep their word `(0, 4)`, which R1/R2 rewrite. `Facts.max_reach`
stays `(0, 4)`. The x86 targets already had it (their `NONE` rows have
`(0, 0)`). The rule, `spec/arch.md` §3.4 "Overlap", bullet **`NONE`**: a
`NONE` record has an empty window on every target, overlaps nothing,
raises no demand and writes nothing; its symbol is still named.

## A `NONE` that names a symbol (measured, all three targets alike)

`.reloc ., R_*_NONE, sym` in `main`'s `.text.main`; GNU ld 2.44, lld 22,
mold 2.37.1, mortar; `-static`.

| question | GNU ld | lld | mold | mortar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--gc-sections`: `sym`'s section (`.data.kept`, nothing else names it) kept | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| control without the `NONE`: kept | no | no | no | no |
| `sym` undefined, named only by `NONE` | `undefined reference` | links | links | `undefined-symbol` |
| `sym` defined in `lib.a(m.o)`: member extracted | yes | yes | yes | yes |

mortar differs from no column where all three agree; on the undefined
row it is GNU ld's. `spec/image.md` I-7 ("every record is an edge,
`R_*_NONE` included") and resolve.md's extraction by symbol table
already say what mortar does; the arch.md bullet states it.

## R4 and TLSDESC with a `NONE` among the records

GAS sorts a section's records by offset (stable), so a `.reloc` on a
member's word lands after that member.

- `NONE` on R4's `nop` (after the `CALL26`, `tls/gd-trad-static-refused.test`
  `overlap.S`): the pattern holds and R4 relaxes; was refused as an
  overlap. GNU ld relaxes; mold keeps GD; all run.
- `NONE` on R4's `add` (between the 514 and the `CALL26`, `between.S`):
  the members are not records i, i+1, i+2; the pattern does not hold.
  Static: mortar refuses by name (`unrecognized general-dynamic TLS
  sequence`); dynamic exec: mortar keeps GD and runs. GNU ld fails an
  assertion (`elfnn-aarch64.c:6764`) in both; lld does not know 513; mold
  keeps GD and runs. The pattern's "exactly records i, i+1, i+2" counts a
  `NONE` as a record (arch.md §3.5's fixed distances).
- Small TLSDESC (R1/R2) with `NONE` on every member's word, members
  interleaved: all four link and run; mortar's output equals its output
  without the `NONE` records. The large form (567/568) only GNU ld and
  mortar's dynamic exec link (lld and mold do not know 568; mortar's
  static refuses `TLSDESC_ADD` by name, as before).

## Other `-r` shapes (item 5)

Over the `-r` outputs of the partial test (three targets, plain, `-g`,
`-ffunction-sections`): GNU ld writes `NONE` on a live record's offset in
`.eh_frame` (1 on AArch64 and x86-64, 2 on i386); lld writes `NONE` in
`.eh_frame` and, with `-g`, in `.debug_info`, `.debug_line`,
`.debug_rnglists`, `.debug_aranges`, none on a shared offset; mold writes
no `NONE` but, with `-g`, 4 non-`NONE` records against symbol 0 per
target. No two non-`NONE` records share an offset; no section's records
are out of offset order. mortar links every one of them and the programs
run. `exp/debug/run.sh` (WP-154's debugging oracle): gdb's transcript of
every mortar output equals the direct GNU ld link's; lldb's differs on
every mortar output, the direct link included, only in naming libc's
frame `__libc_start_main` where GNU ld's output gives
`__libc_start_main_impl` (two symbols at one address): not a `-r` shape.
